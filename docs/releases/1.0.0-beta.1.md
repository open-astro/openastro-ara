# OpenAstro Ara 1.0.0-beta.1

This is a beta, the first public release of OpenAstro Ara. It is for people who want to try the next release early and report problems, so put it only on a rig where a problem tonight is acceptable.

Ara runs your whole imaging night from a small computer at the telescope, such as a Raspberry Pi. The app on your laptop, tablet or phone plans the night and watches it; close the app and the rig keeps imaging.

## Read this first

Everyone who built Ara from source before this release should read these. If this is your first Ara, they still tell you what the rig will ask of you.

- **Update the app and the rig together.** The rig now refuses a settings save from an older app, and Polar Align needs steps an older app cannot send. An older app shows settings it could not save, and your recent and saved targets stop syncing. Build the app and the rig from this same release. The app warns you when their versions do not match.
- **Confirm one meridian flip on each profile.** Any earlier confirmation is cleared. Until you confirm, every flip sends a critical alert and waits 60 seconds before it starts. Watch one flip from start to end, then press **Confirm** in Settings → Unattended running → When to stop.
- **Polar Align now works the way NINA's does.** It no longer sends the mount Home or unparks it. It starts from where you point, turns RA by about 10° twice with the mount's motor (never a GoTo) and plate-solves three times. A mount whose driver cannot turn an axis at a set speed can no longer run Polar Align. Unpark the mount, point the tube 2 to 5° from the pole with the counterweights down, then start.
- **Polar Align asks before the mount moves.** The first motor run on each mount asks how far the counterweight can turn up before anything hits the pier or tripod. Confirm it on the Polar Align card, or in Settings › Imaging › Polar align. Every start then shows which way RA will turn and how far, and nothing moves until you tap **Move the mount**. The first run on a mount also turns RA 0.5° to learn which way the motor turns.
- **Polar Align refuses some starting points.** It will not start on a parked mount, a mount that is slewing, a guider that is guiding, a tube within 3° of the pole, or a mount that reads a Dec past the pole (some do after a sync). Point the tube 2 to 5° from the pole, or tap **Move to start position** in the pre-flight list.
- **Polar Align's first turn goes east in the north and west in the south.** It no longer picks the side from the mount's clock. If that turn would cross the meridian where you point, change Settings › Imaging › Polar align › Turn direction, or point on the other side.
- **One measurement per Start, and Next is gone.** Start measures once, then follows your knob turns until Done or Stop. To check your result, tap **Check** on the adjust screen or after Done.
- **Tracking stays off when Polar Align ends.** To get tracking back after Done or Check, turn off *Stop tracking when polar align ends* in Settings › Imaging › Polar align. A GoTo still turns tracking on by itself.
- **Polar Align holds the mount until it ends.** GoTo, Sync, Center, Park and Home are refused while it runs, and so are a sequence's GoTo, Park, Find Home and flip. Tap Done or Stop first. The run also ends if the mount keeps tracking during the live readout, or if Dec moves during an RA turn. If you see "the mount kept tracking", check that the mount really turns tracking off. If Dec moves, tighten the Dec clutch.
- **An uncertain mount position blocks GoTo and Park.** When Polar Align could not confirm the mount stopped, or the mount reconnected during it, Ara marks the position unverified. GoTo, Park, a new Polar Align and a sequence's moves are refused until you clear it, even after a restart. In the mount window, press Home, or tap "Check position with a plate solve". After a power loss, confirm by eye that the mount is at Home, then confirm Home there. Stop, the direction pad and tracking still work meanwhile.
- **GoTo waits for a trusted clock.** GoTo, Sync, Center and Polar Align wait until the rig's clock is trusted and the mount's clock is set from it. A rig with working internet time needs nothing. At a dark site, open the app once after the rig boots (it sends its time when it connects), or sync the time in Settings. Park and Home are not affected.
- **GoTo refuses low targets under the pole.** On a German equatorial mount, a target close to the lower meridian under the pole would raise the counterweight more than 60°. Ara now refuses that GoTo. Wait until the target rises, or pick another.
- **The Polar Aligner device must confirm it stopped.** If a motorised polar aligner does not report both motors still after Stop, Ara says so and refuses Home and Resume. Press Stop again, or cut its power. A run that needs more than 40 moves, or moves an axis much further than it first measured, now stops. Start again from a pose closer to the pole.
- **Scripts that drive the rig need a look.** If you drive the rig from your own scripts, several Polar Align error reasons and mount refusals have new names, and a Home sent just before a stop is dropped. `CHANGELOG.md` lists each one.

## New things you can do

**Setting up**

- **A setup wizard builds your profile.** It finds your rig on the network, connects your gear through AlpacaBridge, fills in camera and telescope details from the devices, picks the plate-solver star database for your field of view and downloads it. Without the rig it keeps the profile on your device and sends it when the rig connects.
- **Add devices on a fresh rig.** The wizard and Simple Mode list the cameras AlpacaBridge sees and add them for you, or by hand from the bridge's driver list.
- **Several profiles, shared as files.** Keep a profile per rig, export one to share, and import someone else's. A shared profile never carries your saved targets, gear choices or mount clearance.
- **Back up and restore.** Snapshot your settings, keep old snapshots trimmed, and restore from this rig or another.

**Planning**

- **A real planetarium, offline.** The Sky Atlas is a full planetarium with your horizon, your field of view and mosaic grids drawn on the sky. Every catalog ships inside the app, so it works at a dark site with no internet.
- **Tonight's Sky and Tonight's Best.** Targets are ranked for your gear, your site and the rest of tonight. The Moon lowers scores, and you can filter by what is up now or set a magnitude floor. Common names show beside designations, such as NGC 246 (Skull Nebula).
- **Find any target.** Search by any name. Ara answers from its own catalogs first, then asks Simbad through the rig when you are online. You can also type RA and Dec.
- **Plan my night.** Each planned target shows its field with your camera frame, and you can rotate it, turn it into a mosaic or swap it for another candidate.
- **Your own skyline.** Draw your local horizon, and Ara refuses a GoTo below it.
- **The planner knows your sky.** Every frame records its sky background, so the planner learns your site and suggests exposure times. It also knows what you have already captured on each target.

**Imaging and sequences**

- **A full sequencer.** Build sequences with targets, loops, conditions and triggers, or start from a template. Import and export NINA sequence files.
- **Runs pause instead of wasting the night.** A lost guide star, a dropped camera, a failed flip or a full disk pauses the run. It resumes on its own when it can, and asks you when it cannot.
- **Live View and Take One.** Short live frames with star circles, in colour for colour cameras, and single test frames with live progress.
- **Calibration that matches your lights.** Flats that set their own exposure, darks and bias matched to your lights, a dark library, and a reminder to shoot calibration at the end of the night.
- **End the night in one tap.** End Night stops the run and guiding, homes and parks the mount, warms and switches off the cooler and disconnects your gear, even with the app closed.
- **Simple Mode parks at dawn.** A Simple Mode plan parks the mount after the last target and stops at dawn.

**Focusing**

- **Smart Focus.** Autofocus in a few frames instead of a full sweep. It learns which side of focus a blurred star is on, measures your focuser's backlash and checks your collimation for free.
- **Refocus when it matters.** Runs refocus when the temperature changes, after a filter change, and wait out bad sky instead of focusing on clouds.
- **Bahtinov mask and guide camera focus.** Focus with a Bahtinov mask, and focus the guide camera from the same place.
- **Filter offsets.** Measure each filter's focus offset, and Ara applies it on a filter change.

**Plate solving and centering**

- **The plate solver comes with the rig.** The ASTAP solver is built in, uses every core, and its star databases download from the app.
- **Center and Center and Rotate.** Slew, solve and center on a target, and turn the camera rotator to the framed angle. Center after drift keeps long runs on target.
- **Check where the scope points.** The mount window takes one quick frame, solves it and tells you how far you are from the target, with Center and Sync beside it.

**Guiding**

- **Guiding from inside Ara.** Ara drives the OpenAstro Guider: connect it, calibrate, run the Guiding Assistant, pick the guide star and change every guider setting from the app.
- **A guide graph and live status.** A PHD2-style graph with history, and the guider's state on every screen.
- **Guiding keeps itself going.** A lost guide star is recovered by plate solve, a crashed guider restarts, and Dec runaway stops guiding before it ruins frames.
- **No guider? Ara measures your mount.** On an unguided rig, a run first measures how long a sub your mount can hold, and shortens every sub to that.

**Polar Align**

- **Three-point Polar Align, NINA's way.** Uses the guide camera or the main camera, averages several frames per point and shows how sure its reading is, such as "0.2′ ± 1.1′". It says "inside your tolerance" only when the error plus twice its uncertainty fits.
- **Pre-flight checks.** Before you start, the card lists every check the rig makes, and why any fails.
- **Move to start position.** When the tube points too close to the pole, this turns only the Dec axis to about 4° off the pole. RA and the counterweights stay put.
- **Motorised polar aligners.** Ara can drive a motorised polar alignment device and correct the error for you.
- **A second opinion from guiding.** After you image, Ara estimates any polar error left from the guiding drift.

**Mount safety and meridian flips**

- **Automatic meridian flips.** Flips run on their own, with a wait you set before the meridian, and the first flip on a profile tells you when it happens.
- **Stop Mount and Emergency Stop.** Stop Mount halts the mount at once. Emergency Stop stops everything, the camera cooler included. Hold it to confirm, so a stray tap does nothing.
- **A direction pad that cannot run away.** Move the mount by hand at Slow, Medium or Fast, with the arrow keys too. The rig stops the move if the app goes quiet, so a dropped link or a sleeping tablet cannot leave the mount moving.
- **Safety and weather.** Ara reacts to your safety monitor and weather limits, warns about dew, and can park and shut down when a night goes wrong.
- **Alerts on your phone.** Overnight problems can reach your phone, and alarms sound at night.

**Storage**

- **Frames go to a disk you choose.** Pick, check, erase and eject the storage disk from the app. New drives default to exFAT, so you can read them on any computer.
- **Copy to another disk.** Copy the storage drive onto a second drive plugged into the rig. Running it again copies only new frames, and it never deletes anything.
- **Safe erasing.** Before you erase a disk, Ara says how many frames it holds and warns about frames never copied anywhere else.
- **Low disk warnings.** Captures stop before the disk fills, and a run can pause itself when space runs low.
- **Backups while you image.** Frames can stream to your desktop as they are taken.

**Image Library, Stats and Sky Album**

- **A Photos-style Image Library.** Browse frames by night and target, rate and tag them, see stretched previews and export them for processing.
- **Stats.** Charts for guiding, focus and temperature, frame quality, a calendar, achievements and AstroBin export.
- **Sky Album.** Collect cards for the Messier, Caldwell, NGC, IC, Sharpless and RCW objects you have imaged.

**Simple Mode and the phone app**

- **Simple Mode (experimental).** An image-first screen for people who want the rig to just work. The latest frame fills the window and Autopilot walks you through Focus, Polar align and Plan. Turn it on in Settings → System → Experimental.
- **Monitor.** A full-screen, read-only view of the night: what the rig is doing, the target, the next flip, weather, guiding and the last frame.
- **Companion Mode on phones.** On a phone the app opens in Simple Mode. It can send the phone's location and time to the rig, and its safety alarm vibrates.
- **Preferences follow you.** Planetarium and planning choices sync across your devices through the rig.

## Gear

- **Everything through ASCOM Alpaca.** Ara talks to all gear through AlpacaBridge: cameras, mounts, focusers, filter wheels, rotators, domes, switches and power boxes, flat panels, safety monitors and weather sensors.
- **DSLRs.** RAW frames from a DSLR arrive through AlpacaBridge like any other camera.
- **Colour cameras.** Live View, previews and autofocus work on one-shot colour cameras, narrowband filters included.
- **ToupTek cameras.** The cooling fan is switched through the bridge's thermal switch, and starts before the cooler.
- **Manual filter wheels.** Ara asks you to change the filter and waits.
- **Domes.** The dome follows the mount.
- **Motorised polar aligners.** Devices such as OAPA and OpenAstroTracker drive the Polar Align corrections.
- **USB GPS.** A cheap USB GPS dongle on the rig or on the computer running the app keeps the clock and site right.
- **Mount details fill themselves in.** The mount sets an empty focal length and aperture, and your profile's site goes into the mount.

## Things that just work better

- **Errors speak plainly.** Every error says what happened and what to do, and the app speaks Brazilian Portuguese too.
- **Offline at a dark site.** The app works on the rig's own Wi-Fi with no internet, finds the rig by itself, and can update the rig from a package you built.
- **The app remembers.** It reconnects to your last rig and opens on your last profile, and a relaunched app picks up the run in progress.
- **Devices recover by themselves.** A device that drops is reconnected, and a device that comes back later is picked up again. Its chip turns amber while it is in trouble, and the fault log records it.
- **Bug reports carry the whole night.** Support → Send me a bug report can include the rig's logs for the last night or more.
- **Night mode.** A red display for dark sites, on Ctrl+N (⌘N on a Mac).
- **Fits any screen.** The app fits windows from small laptops to 4K, and phones get their own layout.

## Fixes worth knowing about

- **Faint 8-bit frames are saved right.** A frame whose values all fit in 0 to 255, such as a short bias, was multiplied by 256 on some cameras. Frames saved that way before are exact: divide each value by 256.
- **Southern targets slew to the right declination.**
- **A slew or sync just after connecting the mount no longer lands about 0.36° off.**
- **The automatic meridian flip really slews, and is confirmed only after it finishes.**
- **Mount Park works.** It used to fail silently.
- **A GoTo from the mount panel turns tracking on first.**
- **A failed autofocus no longer lets a plan image out of focus.**
- **A lost guide star stops the frame in progress, and the run resumes only once guiding has settled.**
- **Settings never show made-up values.** Each settings page loads from the rig before it saves.
- **Many smaller fixes and internal work** (tests, build checks and cleanup) are listed in `CHANGELOG.md`.

## Known limits in this beta

- **Simple Mode is experimental.** It is off by default on computers and tablets. On a phone it is the only mode.
- **Ara is built for a computer-controlled equatorial mount with guiding.** Star trackers are not supported, and there are no manual or hand-turn modes. Polar Align needs a mount whose motors Ara can turn; it refuses alt-az mounts.
- **Move to start position has been tested on a simulated mount only.** Keep a hand on the mount's power switch the first time you use it.
- **The rig.** The reference rig is a Raspberry Pi 4 (4 GB or more) or Pi 5 on 64-bit Debian Trixie. Other 64-bit ARM boards work best-effort. Guiding needs the OpenAstro Guider on the rig, and your gear needs AlpacaBridge.
- **The app** runs on Windows, macOS 12 (Monterey) or newer, Linux, Android and iOS.

## Building this release

This release is source only. Download `openastro-ara-1.0.0-beta.1-source.tar.gz` and
its `.sha256` from this release's Assets, or clone the tag:

    git clone --branch v1.0.0-beta.1 --recurse-submodules https://github.com/open-astro/openastro-ara.git

Do not use GitHub's "Source code" links: they leave out the parts the build
needs. Then follow docs/RUNNING.md. Build the app and the daemon from this same
release; the app warns you when their versions do not match.

Building needs the .NET 11 SDK and the .NET 10 runtime, and the exact Flutter version the source names. To put the daemon on a Raspberry Pi, build its package from this release as docs/DEPLOY.md shows.

Settings and saved data on the rig survive a rebuild. After you update, confirm one meridian flip on each profile, and confirm the mount's clearance before the first Polar Align.
