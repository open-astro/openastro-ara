Mount by hand: direction pad, Home, Where is it pointing?, Dome follows the mount

v1 (2026-11-11), for #1649 and #1650 (built in their PR); the Dome window is #1689's, a later PR. The pad's W is on the left and E on the right, as in the full UI's pad. The pad works: hold a direction (mouse, touch or the arrow keys once you have clicked the window) and let go to stop. Diagonals move both axes. The rig stops an axis by itself if the app's lease renewals stop.

Pad

Eight directions and a centre Stop, 56 pt cells (over the 44 pt minimum). Labels N, S, E, W as in the Full UI pad. Hold to move, let go to stop; Stop aborts every axis.

Speed

Slow / Medium / Fast: the 5 %, 25 % and 100 % presets of the mount's top speed, or the nearest of its own fixed steps (fewer names when it has fewer steps). Starts on Medium; kept while the window is open only.

Unavailable

The pad greys out with one reason under it; first match wins: parked, Plan imaging, polar alignment, guiding. During a GoTo only Stop works.

Tracking speed

Sidereal, Lunar, Solar (King when the mount offers it), as named segments under Tracking. Only the rates the mount reports; a sidereal-only mount shows no row. Off sidereal, a caption says so. Layout A chosen 2026-11-11.

Home

The pad's centre, a house; while the mount moves on its own it becomes the red Stop square. The Position row says At home. "Homing…" from the tap, "At home" when the mount says so.

Pointing · Check

The mount's RA/Dec with Check beside it, no separate section. One quick frame at the Preview exposure, solved on the rig. Center shows only when more than 1′ off the Target.

Sync

After a good Check, only while nothing runs (no plan imaging, no guiding, mount still): Sync writes the solve into the mount's model. Never automatic. A connected, still rotator is synced to the solved angle by Check itself.

Dome follows the mount

On / Off in the Dome window, only for a dome that can turn. Using the dial, Park, Home or Stop turns following off and says so once.
