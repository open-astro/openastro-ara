Weather window B chosen

The Simple Mode window for a weather station (#1662). Read-only: the station's current readings and how old they are. A reading the station doesn't report has no row or tile. The age counts up live here; switch State to see a stale station, a failed read and a disconnected one.

Readings

The seven the issue names, in this order: cloud cover, sky quality, temperature, dew point, humidity, rain, wind (speed, direction and gusts), then the moon (lit and its phase, computed, not read; added 2026-11-08). Tiles go two across; with an odd count the last one spans the row, so there is never a hole. Pressure, sky temperature and the other computed rows (sun, moon altitude, NELM) stay in the full UI. A NaN or missing reading is left out.

Age

"Updated 8 s ago" under the readings, counting up once a second from the daemon's capture time. The daemon reads every 2 s and Ara asks every 15 s, so a live station never shows more than about 20 s. Past 60 s it turns amber: "Last reading 4 min ago". Under a minute it shows seconds, then minutes, then hours.

Past safety limits

Wind, humidity and the dew-point margin are checked against the rig's safety policy (the same numbers the full UI's Thresholds rows show). A reading past its limit turns amber, so you see why the safety monitor might say Unsafe. Only when "Weather triggers safety" is on.

Read failed / not connected

A failed read says "Sensor read failed — check the device." in the error colour and shows no numbers, so old readings can't pass for live ones. Not connected shows only "Not connected". Connect stays on the Devices row in Settings.

Icons

Material outlined icons in Text Secondary, amber with the tile when past a limit; screen readers skip them. Cloud, stars (sky quality), thermometer, dew point, drop (humidity), a cloud with rain (rain), air (wind), crescent (moon). Chosen: in front of the value, 22 pt like the number (user, 2026-11-08). Every tile is the same height (80 pt), room for the number and two caption lines; a longer caption ends in an ellipsis.

No pill, no controls

The weather station has nothing to move, so no status pill (#1668) and no plan guard. Everything here is a reading.

Stays in the full UI

"Weather triggers safety", the wind / humidity / dew-point limits, auto-connect, and the computed sky rows.
