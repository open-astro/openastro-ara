Simple Focus: the guide camera

Focusing the guide camera in Simple Mode: its own live HFR readout while you turn the guide scope's focuser (or slide the camera in its draw tube; an off-axis guider's helical focuser), with the full UI's turn advice. The guide camera's frames fill the screen behind the panels while it runs, as Manual's Bahtinov crop does. Pick where the choice lives: A, a Main telescope / Guide camera row above Auto / Manual; B, a third segment beside them. Everything is on view; nothing folds away. proposed

Notes for the decision

• The shutter starts and stops the guide camera's live focus, as it does Smart Focus and the Bahtinov readout.
• The readout, the turn advice ("Keep going — same way you just moved, a small move") and the in-focus target come from the full UI's guide-focus card, translated: one source of truth.
• Blockers say what to do in the panel: connect the guider, or stop guiding first (the guider owns its camera while it loops or guides). With an off-axis guider the main telescope comes first, with "Already in focus" for someone who focused by hand.
• Left out: the full UI's Guiding Assistant button (a full-UI tool).
