Simple Focus: Smart Focus result and the Bahtinov helper

One Focus panel, two methods (the chip on the right rail: Auto / Manual). Auto shows the Smart Focus result the full UI shows (headline, HFR, stats, the V-curve, the weak-fit warning), all on view: nothing folded away; Manual is the Bahtinov mask helper, its spikes drawn in the zoomed star under the status pill, with the mask-off question asked inside the panel (Simple Mode has no popups). Pick a result layout for Auto: A grows the Focus panel (left of the right rail, as today), B keeps the panel small and puts the curve in the bottom row. Parts tagged proposed are new to Simple Mode.

Behind the panels is the frame Simple Mode already shows full screen. With "stars circled" on, the stars the focus run measured are ringed on that frame (green: kept, grey: dropped) instead of a second thumbnail, like the full UI's FOCUSED image.

Notes for the decision

• Everything Auto shows comes from the same pure helpers the full UI uses (headline, weak-fit text, step size text, the V-curve chart), already translated: one source of truth.
• Guide camera focus (the full UI's second tab) is left out of Simple Mode: it's a once-per-setup task, not a nightly one (4×90 rule). Say if you want it.
• Manual's turn advice (Make a move / Keep going / Go back / In focus) and the px readout are the full UI's Bahtinov card logic; Finish asks for the mask to come off inside the panel, and Simple Mode won't start a frame or a plan with the mask still marked on.
• The shutter is Start/Stop in both methods, as today.
