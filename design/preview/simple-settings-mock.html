Simple Mode's own Settings: sheet B, one window per device, bare minimum proposed

The gear opens Simple Mode's Settings: only what the top 90 % of nights touch, never the full UI's form. Your pick B: a sheet over the image, sections on the left. Each device has its own window, and the same window opens from its dot in the top bar (toggle "From the device dot"): one piece of code, two places. iPad 6 at 1024×768, drawn at 75 %. Trimmed to the bare minimum for a successful night (KISS). Everything here is a mock; the values are examples.
