Simple Mode Settings: one place for each thing proposed

KISS, no knobs, no repeats. Each device's window opens from its own dot in the top bar, and only there; the gear keeps what belongs to no device: the rig, tonight, and the way to change rig or profile. With the device windows gone from it, Settings is one short panel again, no sheet. iPad 6 at 1024×768, drawn at 75 %; values are examples.
