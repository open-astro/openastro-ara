Simple Mode's own Settings: Ara decides (KISS) proposed

The gear opens Simple Mode's Settings: only what the top 90 % of nights touch, never the full UI's form. Your pick B: a sheet over the image, sections on the left. Each device has its own window, and the same window opens from its dot in the top bar (toggle "From the device dot"): one piece of code, two places. iPad 6 at 1024×768, drawn at 75 %. KISS: no knobs; automation and self-recovery do the work. Each place says what it is doing now and what Ara does for you; the only buttons are Connect, Park now and changing rig or profile. Everything here is a mock; the values are examples.

The values Ara uses (−10 °C, 2 °C, dither, dawn) are the profile's, set once in the setup wizard or the Advanced UI; Simple Mode reads them and never asks. Each "Ara decides" line is a claim to check against the daemon before it ships: a line Ara doesn't do yet becomes daemon work, not a knob.

ASIAir's settings were the reference for the Gear details (its main camera, mount with meridian flip, EAF autofocus triggers, guiding dither, storage and Wi-Fi pages). Wi-Fi and power outputs aren't here: the rig's network is the Pi's, and Ara has no power-port control yet.
