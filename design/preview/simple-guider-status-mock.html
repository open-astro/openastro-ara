One rule for exposure, gain and offset in Simple Mode only (chosen 14 Nov): the label is the plain word, the unit lives in the value, and the value uses one format. Advanced Mode keeps its own labels ("Exposure (s)", "Default exposure (s)" and so on).

Label"Exposure", "Gain", "Offset". No unit and no "time" or "Default" in the label; the section title says it is a default.
Value"0.5 s", "5 s", "90 s", "2 min". Unit always in the value, with a space. No "1.0", no bare "5".
Typed fieldPlaceholder is the current or default value in the same format, greyed ("5 s"). The suffix shows the unit while typing.
Gain and offsetBare numbers ("100", "50"): they have no unit. The guider's own exposure reads "Auto".
