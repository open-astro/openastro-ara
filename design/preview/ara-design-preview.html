Simple Mode, two rails (ALP-4810, the prototype on feat/simple-mode): the frame fills the screen; the strip on top shows connected gear, then Search, Night mode, Show all options and Settings; the camera status pill sits top-left (at most 300 px) and Info is one line top-right. The right rail holds the mode, the shutter and the settings chip, only as tall as its controls; Stop sits alone at the bottom right (2s hold). The left rail holds Sky, Info and Library. Guiding and the histogram share the bottom row, each once it has data. Popovers and mode panels open left of the right rail and never move it; Run's plan and Night done are a sheet centred over the image. Click the mode name, the settings chip or a mode in the grid; click the image to close a popover, or with none open to switch the immersive view.
