Rotator window: the sky-angle dial proposed

Layout A matches the Focuser window: the sky angle big on the left, with the mechanical angle under it when they differ, and the dial on the right (180 pt so both fit the 360 pt window). Layout B is the first mock, with the dial centred and the angle in its middle. The round dial from today's Focuser window becomes the Rotator's. Its face is a 0–360° sky-angle scale. The number under the blue mark is where the rotator is, or, while you turn, where it will go. Each click is one step of the size picked under the dial, and the angle snaps to that step. The rotator moves when you let go. The middle shows the sky angle, and the mechanical angle under it when they differ. Everything works: turn the dial (drag round it, scroll over it, or focus it and use the arrow keys) and watch the dashed frame on the image turn with it.

Stop (chosen)

The middle of the dial is the shutter's stop square and nothing else: no word under it. At rest the square is grey, dimmed, and takes no tap. While the rotator turns (a move from this window, a plan or the full UI) it turns red, and a tap stops the rotator where it is. The sky angle on the left then reads where it stopped. Turn the dial and let go, then tap Stop mid-turn to try it.

Middle of the dial

1 Last frame: the latest image, turned the way it will come out at the angle under the mark, so you frame on the real target. Empty-sky placeholder before the first frame. 2 Frame outline: the frame as it sits on the sky, with the new framing in green over the current one while you turn. 3 North / East: where north and east will point in the image. 4 Flip 180°: a button that turns the frame half a turn, a common re-frame and the angle after a meridian flip. 5 Frame + Stop: the outline at rest, and a red stop square while it turns (needs a halt the rotator's notifier doesn't have yet). 6 Target angle and 7 Solid face as before.

Turning

Turning the face clockwise brings lower numbers under the mark, the way a printed scale works. Up and Right arrows, and a screen reader's increase, add one step.

While a plan runs

Letting go asks first, in the window: "A plan is running. Rotate anyway?" with Cancel and Rotate anyway, the way Mount's Park and the filter wheel do.

Status pill

"Rotator · Moving…" in blinking green while it turns, whoever turned it (#1668). Tapping it opens this window.
