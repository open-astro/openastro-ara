Rig Window Altitude Scenario

No slot for gear the rig lacks

With no Safety monitor and no weather station, their tiles are not drawn and the grid reflows: the altitude graph takes two columns and two rows, HFR and Stars grow to two rows, and the meridian flip moves beside the timeline that shows it. Switch Rig to compare with the full grid.

Now, without the hole

The countdown keeps its own width and Up next takes the rest of the tile, so the Mac window has no empty area between them. Switch Window to check both sizes.

Colour camera text

With no filter wheel the filter is left out, separator included: "NGC 1269 · 60 s" and "Frame 28", not "NGC 1269 · · 60 s" and "Frame 28 ·". The single frames bar needs no filter name.

One way to count frames

"Frame 27 of 120" is the frame being exposed. The bar counts saved frames and says so: "26 saved". Today the same tile shows 27 of 120 beside a bare 26/120.

Target named once per tile

A long name that equals the short one once spaces are removed ("NGC 1269" and "NGC1269") is not shown. The frames line drops "on NGC 1269" when the tile already names the target.

Altitude graph

Under the curve: the shaded area is the time this target is imaged, the dot is now, the dashed line is 30°, and the axis names the plan's start and end. The full-height band is what ships today; it reads as a selected column.

Guider on a fixed scale

The live trace always spans ±2.5″, with the axis labelled, so a calm night and a runaway look different. Today the trace stretches to its own peak. RA and Dec have a key.

Stop says how

"hold 2 s" under STOP at rest. Today the hold only shows once the button is pressed. Monitor's 108 pt Stop only; the 60 px Stop elsewhere is unchanged.
