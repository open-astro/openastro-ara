Focuser window: coarse and fine knobs proposed

The round dial goes to the Rotator. The Focuser gets two upright knurled knobs side by side, like a dual-speed focuser seen from the side: a big coarse knob and a small fine one. Roll a knob up to go in and down to go out, the way a real focuser's drawtube travels. Each ridge is one click and gives a haptic tick. A click scales to the focuser's travel: coarse is about 1 % of it and fine a tenth of that, so 300 and 30 steps for this 30 000-step focuser. A relative focuser, which has no travel, falls back to 100 and 10. A mouse or trackpad scroll over a knob rolls it too, and the focuser moves once the scrolling stops. The target shows while you roll, and the focuser moves when you let go. The two knobs replace the 10 / 100 / 1000 step picker. Everything here works: drag a knob, use the mouse wheel over it, or focus it and press the arrow keys. The star sharpens near best focus (12 400).
