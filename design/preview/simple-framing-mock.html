Frame a Target in Simple Mode mock

Issue #1899. Pick a Target, switch Framing on, turn the camera box on the sky, queue it. The angle is degrees east of north, the same number the Full UI Planning tab uses. Frame (a) is built in slice 2; (b) to (d) are for reviewing the whole flow and are built in slice 3. Everything here is drawn from one angle value; try the dial, the steppers and Snap in (a).

The box is the camera's field on the profile optics (as in the Full UI), drawn on the bare sky. Dragging the dial, the − and + steppers (1° per tap, hold to repeat) and Snap all move the same angle. Snap sets the catalogue position angle of the object (35° for M 31); it is disabled when the catalogue has none. Picking another Target sets Framing back to Off and the angle to 0°; picking the same Target again keeps both. Off means a plain centred GoTo, On means Center and Rotate at this angle, 0° included.

A queued Target keeps the framing it was planned with; later changes on the sky never rewrite it. Clear (red) removes it and the block goes back to a plain centred GoTo. The off-centre marker only shows when the box centre is not on the catalogue centre.
