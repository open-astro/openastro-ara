The options

How it sets a valueGood atWeak at
Telescope A · StepperTap − / + for 1 mm; hold and it repeats, speeding up to 10 mm, then 50 mm steps.Small corrections (a reducer: 480 → 510). One control, no popup.Big jumps from nothing (0 → 2000 mm is a long hold), though the mount usually fills it first.
Telescope B · Digit wheelsTap the value; one wheel per digit opens under it, like the iOS timer. Tap again to close; it saves on close.Any exact number in a few flicks (2350 is four).Takes room while open; a digit at a time is fiddly with gloves.
Site A · Location only"Use this iPad's location" fills latitude, longitude and elevation. Coordinates only read out; elevation has a stepper (the iPad's height is often off).The 90 %: you're at the rig with the iPad. Nothing to type, nothing to get wrong.An iPad with location off, or setting up a remote site: send them to the full UI (Simple never links there, so they'd switch out).
Site B · Location + AdjustAs A, plus Adjust opens digit wheels for latitude and longitude (N/S, E/W wheel, degrees, 4 decimals).Location off or a remote site without leaving Simple Mode.More controls on the page; most nights never touch it.

My pick: Telescope A (the mount fills the usual case; the stepper fixes reducers and odd scopes) and Site A (4×90 %: location covers the night at the rig). Elevation gets the stepper in both, whole metres.
