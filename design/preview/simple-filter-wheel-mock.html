Filter wheel window: the wheel itself proposed

The Focuser got knobs and the Rotator got a dial, each shaped like the hardware, with the reading big on the left. These layouts give the Filter wheel the same treatment. Today's list (#1666) is here for comparison. Everything works: tap a filter (or roll the drum in C), switch to Plan running or a manual wheel to see the asks, and try 5, 7, 8 and 12 slots.

A · Wheel beside the reading, tinted (chosen 2026-11-07)

The Focuser and Rotator layout: the filter in place big on the left ("Ha", "Slot 5" under it, then "Moving to OIII…" in blinking green), the wheel on the right at 180 pt. Each filter is a cell on the wheel's face, and the cells are the only way to pick one (user, 2026-11-07): tap one and the wheel turns the short way until that cell sits under the blue mark, where the light passes. The tap is the action, as today: no Start, because a filter change is one short move. Cells show the filter's name when it fits (four letters), otherwise the slot number. Up to 8 cells fit at 44 pt; a 12-slot wheel shows as B.

B · Big wheel, name in the hub

The wheel takes the window's width (260 pt), so 12 cells still get 44 pt targets. The filter in place sits in the hub, the slot numbers are engraved on the face beside the cells, and the status line runs under the wheel. Reads well but breaks the "reading left, control right" pattern the other two windows share.

C · Drum, edge-on

The wheel seen from the side, rolled like the Focuser's knobs: drag or scroll it until a filter sits between the blue marks, let go and it changes (tap a row to go straight there). It wraps round, as a wheel does. Closest to the knobs' feel, but rolling is slower than one tap, and only five filters show at once.

Glass colours

Tinted cells colour each filter as its glass (L clear, R red, Ha deep red, OIII teal…), from the name; an unknown name stays plain. The tint is content, like the image, never a state: the blue mark and ring say which filter is in, green says moving, amber says swap. Night mode reds everything anyway, and Plain is there if tints feel off-brand.

Asks and manual wheels

Unchanged from #1666, in the new place. While a plan runs, a tap rings the cell in grey and asks "A plan is running. Change the filter anyway?" with Cancel and Change anyway. A manual wheel rings the cell in amber with "Swap in Ha, then confirm." and Cancel swap / Ha is in; the wheel turns only once the user confirms.

Access and text size

Every cell is a button read as "5 · Ha" (selected for the one in place, disabled while the wheel turns), so the tab order and screen reader match today's list. The wheel and its cells grow with the text, as the knobs and dial do. Per-filter focus offsets stay out of the window: the daemon never applies them (#1667).
