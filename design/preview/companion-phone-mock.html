What's the same as the iPad

  • The same sections: Preview, Focus, P.A., Guider and Plan, plus Sky, Info and Library.
  • The same STOP: 60 px, red, a 2 s hold with a filling ring. It's on every screen in both layouts, including Settings and over every panel.
  • Plan squares in the bottom-right corner, fact tiles in the bottom left.
  • No dialogs, sheets or snackbars. Questions are pills, and panels open in place.

What's different on a phone

  • No way into the full UI. The strip has search, Night mode and Settings, and no Full UI button.
  • One status card, top left (chosen 2026-11-11): the camera status pill and the message pills merge into one card. The camera status is its first line, and results, errors and questions stack under it. The iPad and desktop get the same card (#1782).
  • Panels take the full width, between the strip and the controls, and scroll inside.
  • Layout B moves both rails into one deck at the bottom, where your thumb is. Section names sit in a row you can swipe, like a camera app. Sky, Info and Library are on the left, the shutter and chip in the middle, STOP on the right. Layout A is today's narrow layout, with the rails on the screen's edges.
  • Location pill: when the rig has no recent time sync, a pill offers this phone's location and time. Not now holds until the next session.
  • Safety alarm: sound and vibration on top of today's full-screen alarm.
  • In landscape, today's narrow layout stays as it is (not drawn here).
