Focus settings in Simple Mode (ara#1544)

Working mock. Simple Mode gets only the Simple tier, in the Focuser window, in place of today's "Refocus every" slider: autofocus during plans on or off, a Keep focus sharp preset that sets all four refocus triggers, and the AF filter. Exposure, binning, step size and each trigger stay in Advanced Mode's Settings, which gets the tiers, the one Advanced toggle and the derived values. The numbers are examples.

The presets set Ara's four triggers. Relaxed: filter change, 3 °C, 25 % HFR, 4 h. Normal: filter change, 2 °C, 15 % HFR, 2 h (today's defaults). Tight: filter change, 1 °C, 10 % HFR, 1 h. When Advanced Mode's values match none of them, Simple Mode shows Custom and picking a preset overwrites them. The AF filter row is hidden without a filter wheel. Bahtinov and guide focus take their exposure from the AF exposure, and the server falls back to the profile when a request leaves it out. "Re-focus after meridian flip" stays in Safety.
