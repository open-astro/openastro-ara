Mount limits in Simple Mode: lowest altitude, highest altitude, flip after the meridian

v1 (2026-11-19). A Limits group in the Mount window, under the Meridian flip switch. The steppers work, the arc follows them, and the buttons in the Try row show what the user sees when a limit is hit. Advanced Mode is unchanged.

Lowest altitude

The profile's flat horizon (today 20° by default, Advanced › Settings › Site). 0° to 60° in 5° steps. Plans already skip targets under it and Tonight's Best greys them. New: a GoTo under it is refused with the reason. With a measured horizon the row is read-only and says where to change it.

Highest altitude

For a camera or rotator that can hit the tripod near the zenith. 60° to 85° in 5° steps, then Off (the default). A GoTo above it is refused. When tracking climbs to it, Ara stops tracking, pauses the plan and says why. It never slews on its own. Needs the server limit from #1589.

At the meridian (flip Auto)

One signed stepper, −30 to +30 min in 5 min steps. Before (−): stop tracking that early, wait for the meridian to pass, then flip; the server already does this as PauseTimeBeforeMeridian. After (+): keep tracking that long past it, then flip (MinutesAfterMeridian; the window's end follows as + 5). Only one of the two is ever non-zero.

Stop before meridian (flip Off)

Off, or 5 to 30 min. Stops tracking and pauses the plan that long before the meridian, and says why. Needs server work: today nothing stops a mount with the flip off except its own firmware.

Arc

Horizon on the left, zenith at the top. Green is where the mount may point; red is outside a limit. Drawn from the same values as the steppers, so the numbers and the picture never disagree.

Saving

Each tap saves at once, like the Meridian flip switch: a pill confirms, a failed save puts the old value back and says so. Lowest must stay at least 15° under highest; the stepper stops there.

Where else

The same window shows in Settings › Mount (it is reused there), so the limits are in both places with no second copy.
