Focuser zero and calibration

Three ways to add "set zero" and "calibrate travel" to Simple Mode's Focuser window. The buttons are live: try them. Example rig: a ZWO EAF at 12 480 steps of 60 000.

A · In the window, under the reading

Fastest to reach. One quiet link under the position, with an inline question. It sits on the window used all night, next to the knobs, for an action done once a season.

B · Settings › Focuser, a Position section Pick

Recommended. The night window stays knobs and Autofocus. Zero and travel calibration live with the focuser's other setup (backlash, versions), where a rare action that moves every saved position belongs. Each row shows only when the driver offers it.

C · Calibrate travel, running

The run itself, for a focuser that finds its own stops (Celestron focus motor today). It shows in place of B's row while it runs; the knobs and Autofocus lock until it ends.

What zero changes

  • Moves: the last autofocus position, the focus-vs-temperature model and any plan step "Move focuser to N".
  • Keeps: filter offsets and backlash, because they are differences, not positions.
  • Can't zero: a relative focuser or a driver without the action. The row is hidden, not greyed out.

What it needs below the UI

  • ASCOM has no "set zero". IFocuser has only Move and Halt. It must go through the driver's Action, as the mount spec sheet does.
  • AlpacaBridge: the ZWO EAF, ToupTek AAF and iOptron iEAF SDKs can zero, but their drivers list no actions today. The Celestron driver already offers Calibrate, AbortCalibration and CalibrationStatus.
