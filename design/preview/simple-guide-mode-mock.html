Guide mode, Guider Camera, Main Camera proposed

Guiding becomes a mode on the right rail, next to Preview, Focus, Polar align and Plan. Its panel shows how guiding goes; the guider's device window becomes Guider Camera (no State, no dither) and Camera becomes Main Camera. Dashed outlines mark what's new. Tap the shutter to start and stop guiding.
