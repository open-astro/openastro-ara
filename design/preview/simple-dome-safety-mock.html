Dome: an all-sky clamshell, from the front

v12 (user, 2026-11-08, Baader AllSky front photo; no sky disc or scope inside, user): the sphere seen from the front, on its base (sloped shoulders, straight sides, a round plinth) with the door running from the sphere down to the plinth. Each side of the top is two nested shells fanned on a bolt just above the centre. Closed, the outer shells meet at the crown and the inner ones cover the band below. Opening, both fan outward and down, the outer one twice as fast, so a V opens at the top and widens until the shells lie stacked over the lower half, stopping with their lower edges on the base's shoulders (user), and the top is open; closing reverses it. The drawing no longer turns with the azimuth: the dial from above shows where the opening points. A roll-off roof is a shed whose roof slides off to the side.

Turning the dome

A: the side view shows the shutter, the dial from above turns the dome, both 150 pt side by side under the reading. Drag round the dial (or scroll, or arrow keys) to a target: a dashed green slit and "→ 245°" beside the azimuth; the middle's green play turns it there, and is the red stop square while it turns, as the rotator dial. B: one 170 pt dial does both: the slit's dark part grows in from the wall as the shutter opens (no side view). C: the side view only, with ◀ ▶ in 5° steps and "Turn to 245°". While a plan runs, a turn asks first. Uses the daemon's existing slew; no daemon change.

Opening and closing, timed

The daemon says only "shutter moving", not how far. Ara knows which way (from what was asked, or the state before), so the window says "Opening…" or "Closing…" and slides the shutter over the time this dome took last time, timed from the last full move it saw and kept on this device; 60 s until it has seen one. If the dome is slower, the shutter waits just short of the end until it reports done; if faster, it snaps there. Reduce motion: no slide, it jumps.

Why unsafe

An ASCOM safety monitor reports only safe or unsafe, never why: the cause is inside the device (its own cloud, rain or wind limits). So the window names the device and says it doesn't give a reason. What Ara can add: a weather reading past its safety limit (when "Weather triggers safety" is on), the same check the daemon makes and the Weather window marks in amber. That one shows even while the monitor reads Safe, since Ara then treats the rig as unsafe too.

What Ara does

Read-only lines from the safety policy, shown while unsafe: "On unsafe: pause the plan, park and close the dome", then "Resumes 10 min after Safe" or "Stays paused until you resume". The policy loads when the window opens and is never written here; it is edited in the full UI.

Moving

"Moving…" blinking green while it turns, parks or finds home; "Opening…" / "Closing…" with a blue ring dot while the shutter goes (a change between two states). Segments and Park / Home lock; Stop never locks. While a plan runs, a shutter change, Park or Home asks first; Stop never asks.

Stays in the full UI

Dome: slew to azimuth, sync, set park here, auto-connect, slaving. Safety: editing On unsafe, auto-resume and its delay, auto-connect.
