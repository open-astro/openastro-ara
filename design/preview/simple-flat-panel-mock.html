Flat panel window B chosen

The Simple Mode window for a cover/calibrator (#1662). Cover Open / Closed, light On / Off, and a brightness slider sent when you let go. Everything works: the cover takes about 3 s to move and the light warms up for about 2 s, and the status pill top right shows both (#1668). Layout A is three plain rows, like the Camera window. Layout B adds a drawing of the panel face beside the cover and light rows, the way the Filter wheel window shows its wheel: the lids slide apart as the cover opens and the face lights up with the brightness.

Cover

Open / Closed segments. While the cover moves the row reads "Moving…" in blinking green and both segments are locked: the cover is one motor. A panel without a cover has no Cover row.

Light

On / Off segments, turning on at the slider's level. While the panel warms up the row reads "Warming up…" in blue, and the pill says "Light warming up…" so you don't shoot flats too early. A dust cover with no light has no Light or Brightness row.

Brightness

0 to the panel's own maximum (255 here), with the level beside the slider as a percent of that maximum (Ara still sends the panel's own units). It is sent when you let go. Letting go above 0 with the light off turns it on at that level; 0 turns it off. Until the panel reports its maximum the slider is disabled and reads "—".

While a plan runs

Any change asks first, in the window: "A plan is running. Change the flat panel anyway?" with Cancel and Change anyway, as the Filter wheel and Rotator windows do. Nothing is sent until you confirm.

Status pill

"Flat panel · Cover moving…" in blinking green, or "Flat panel · Light warming up…" with a blue ring. Cover movement wins when both are true. It shows whoever moved it, and a tap opens this window (#1668).

Stays in the full UI

Auto-brightness target, auto-flats and the flat-panel safety policies. Connect / Disconnect stay on the Devices row in Settings.
