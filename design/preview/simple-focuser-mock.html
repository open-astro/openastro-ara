All three: Step 10 / 100 / 1000, the position updates live, "Moving to 12 500…" in amber while it travels, and Refocus when it cools by 0.5 °C steps (Off at 0). Screen readers get Move in / Move out buttons on A and B. My pick: A · Knob, as you suggested; B is the fallback on a phone.
