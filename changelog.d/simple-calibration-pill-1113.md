### Changed (Simple Mode)

- **Darks folded by gain and temperature.** Calibration › Darks & bias lists one row per gain and temperature, with the number of darks, the exposure range, the newest age and the worst set's tag; a tap opens it. The pair the queued plan needs opens by itself, so a big library no longer runs past the panel.

### Fixed (Simple Mode)

- **Guide Camera files shown as unknown, not missing.** When the rig can't read the Guider's dark library and bad-pixel map status (the Guider restarting, a failed read, or the first read still out), Calibration › Guide Camera says so with Try again, instead of showing them as Not built with Build buttons. The tab reads the files again when it opens and when the Guider comes back.
- **Status card under the top strip during Preflight.** The status card and its pills stay under the top strip beside the Preflight steps when the window has room, instead of always dropping below the steps.
