### Changed

- The cooling fan interlock now follows a fan you choose instead of a device called "Thermal Switch": pick the fan's switch and port under **Cooling fan** in the Camera window, the setup wizard or the camera settings. It works on any driver and survives renaming the device. Existing profiles have no fan chosen, so no fan runs with the cooler until you choose one: Ara asks once and logs it when cooling starts (#1700).
- Emergency Stop now stops the mount where it is: it aborts any slew or manual move and switches tracking off. It no longer parks, because a park is a slew that can repeat the fault you pressed Stop for. The API field `park_requested` is now `mount_stopped` on this path, and the failed step `park` is now `stop_mount` (#1672).
- The architecture decisions now live in `docs/adr/`: nine accepted records cover the daemon and client split, Alpaca-only equipment, client-side planning, the trusted-LAN single-client model, the Wayland-only Linux client, ASTAP, GPS on the client and Simple Mode, and three proposed records (0011 to 0013) from an architecture review describe code that does not exist yet. `design/PORT_PLAYBOOK.md`, `design/README.md`, the Simple Mode brief and the Sky Album spec point at the records instead of repeating them.

### Fixed

- A running plan's frame count no longer drops to 0: each live progress update reset it, and progress updates now carry the count.
- Simple Mode plans no longer stop to ask for a filter change on a rig without a filter wheel.
- Simple Mode no longer shows the "Capture calibration frames tonight?" popup when a plan starts; an unanswered run ends without flats, as before, and a pill now says so and where to change it.
- Simple Mode: a target picked in Search now becomes the plan's target at once, and an object outside tonight's ranked list shows its real altitude instead of being called below the horizon.
- A guide-camera focus loop left running can always be stopped: Simple Mode's Focus opens on Guide when it finds one running, Preview's Guide row stays selectable, and Emergency Stop now ends the loop too. Emergency Stop also stops the Bahtinov readout, a running autofocus and polar alignment, and says in words which of these failed to stop.
- Simple Mode: every floating panel and message pill now has a 1 px Hairline contour, so a panel no longer disappears when the frame or dark area behind it matches its colour. The line is drawn on top of the panel and moves nothing; a warning or error pill keeps its own colour.
- Simple Mode: the Focus, Polar align and Guider panels now stop above the guiding and histogram row, as Plan's sheet does, instead of running behind it, and the Guider panel is centred in that space like Plan's.
- Simple Mode: VoiceOver names the Guider panel's Gain, RA and Dec sliders, and picking an object in Plan's search puts the keyboard away.
- Simple Mode: the autofocus V-curve in the Focus panel is now tall enough to read. The chart was 80 pt tall and its axes left the plot only 18 pt, so the curve was flat and the HFR labels collided; it is now 160 pt, as in the full UI.
- After you create a profile that becomes active, the Guider now moves to that profile's guider twin. If its guider settings are refused, Start guiding, polar alignment, the dark library and the guide-camera focus loop refuse at once and say why, instead of using the previous profile's Guide Camera (#1665).
- A start of guiding that fails now says why: the Guider's reason (for example its Guide Camera not connected) shows in a pill and the start ends at once instead of waiting out the 30 s guide-star timeout (#1664). A settle timeout after guiding has begun does not fail the start.
- Simple Mode: a device window, the OpenAstro Ara sheet and Search no longer stay open on top of each other on a wide window (#1674). Opening one closes the others; before, the Ara sheet could hide a device window's labels and controls, such as a switch's On.
- Emergency Stop now halts a turning rotator where it is (#1671). Before, the stop never reached the rotator: a move started from a window kept going, and a plan's rotate only stopped being waited on. A rotator that refuses the halt is called out as "ROTATOR HALT FAILED — it may still be turning". The Emergency Stop confirmation lists the rotator among what it does.
- Polar Align's rough step now measures the mount axis instead of reading the main camera's pointing at Home as the axis (#1727). On the first real night the rough readout went to zero while the fine step then found the axis 75′ away, because a camera is never exactly on the axis at Home (cone error, the Home position). The rough step now slews to Dec 85°, turns 60° in RA with the main camera solving at each end, fits the axis from the two frames and follows the knobs from there — the same routine as the fine step, on the main camera. It opens in the `seeding` state like the fine step; `adjusting` follows the fit. The fine step that follows it goes Home again before its own turn, so the two turns never add up toward a mount's limit; with *Use Home* on, every fine start does, on any mount (an *Adjust again* after the check starts from the check's last turn; a mount without Find Home is asked). A rough-step start with the main camera disconnected is refused at once instead of homing, slewing and then blaming the sky; **Done** on the check's verdict now ends the routine (the panel used to stay on the verdict); Simple Mode's *Check* enables at the tolerance exactly as the Full UI does, and its side-of-the-pole sentence is translated. Without Home in use, each later step slews back to where the run started before its turn; the check's *Disagreement* is the distance between the two measured axes (a fine readout of the same size on the other side of the pole no longer reads as agreement); **Next** no longer waits for a frame in flight; and a new step clears the previous step's readout, so the Setup checklist cannot call the mount aligned on the rough step's number. The check refuses a verdict when its three solves do not describe a turn about one axis (a slipping clutch or a stalled motor that the mount still reports as a turn), a Home set by hand moves off the axis relative to the mount's reading, a rough start with the main optics unset is refused before the mount moves, and a stored turn or exposure value outside its range is clamped instead of blocking the panel's edits. Simple Mode's Start still polar-aligns on a rig whose main camera is off: a start that names no step begins at the fine step when the rough step cannot run. With Home in use every fine start goes Home first, a mount without Find Home asks for it, and Stop no longer leaves the panel waiting for Home. A new step clears the previous step's readout on the daemon as well as in the app, the Continue button cannot go missing on a mount without Find Home (the started event now always comes first), and a solver timeout no longer ends the routine silently. A rough start while a live view or a plan holds the main camera is refused before the mount moves (Simple Mode's unnamed start goes to the fine step instead); after a Home set by hand the guide camera's solves are hinted at the pole, not at the mount's stale reading; a turn or exposure value stored by an older build is clamped before it reaches the mount; Night Setup no longer restarts the routine when you come back to a finished check; and Simple Mode's check verdict is translated. Emergency Stop now stops the routine while it homes or runs the check, not only in the adjust loop; the rough step's live frames take one quick look each instead of a minutes-long blind search when a frame fails; Done is refused in the rough step (Next or Stop); and the Setup checklist marks a finished check outside the green zone as needing work rather than untouched. The client no longer asks the guider for a live picture during the rough step (its frames are the main camera's), and the Setup checklist does not count the rough step's green readout as aligned (#1729).
- The daemon keeps one log: Polar Align runs and every plate solve are now in `logs/` and in the bug report, not a separate `NINA/Logs` file (#1591). Core log lines now read `File.cs|Member|42|message`, without quotes around each value.
