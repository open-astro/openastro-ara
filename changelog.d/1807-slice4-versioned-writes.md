### Changed (Settings)
- **Every settings write sends the version it was loaded with** (#1807): the setup wizard, a profile saved offline and applied on reconnect, and the recent and saved targets now read each profile section with its version and send that version back with the save, as the Settings panels and Simple Mode already do. When another device saved the target list in between, the app reads it again and merges once more; if it is refused again, the targets stay on this device for the next sync. A save the rig refuses for want of a version says "Reload these settings from the rig before saving" and offers Reload. On a rig that sends no version (one older than the 1.0 betas) the app now refuses to save instead of writing over the rig's copy.
