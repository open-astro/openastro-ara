### Fixed (cooling)

- **Ara warns when a cooler it switched on does not respond, and a Cool Camera step fails after a minute** — camera cooling (#2076): when `CoolerOn` reads back off for a minute after Ara switched the cooler on, or it reads on at 0 % power with the sensor well above its set-point and not moving (a camera with no 12 V), the daemon logs a warning, posts one notify-only `value_mismatch` camera fault ("Cooler not responding: is the camera's 12 V connected?"), and sets the new optional `cooler_not_responding` field on the camera runtime. Simple Mode shows an amber camera pill and a caption under the Cooler row, and the preflight cooler item gives the same hint. Nothing re-sends the cooler write. A plan's Cool Camera step whose cooler reads off for a minute, in the ramp or the wait, now publishes that warning (if the refresh pass has not yet) and then fails through the normal step failure with the 12 V hint, instead of after the 10-minute regulating bound; an Emergency Stop or a REST cooler-off is not counted, and a Warm Camera step is not affected.
