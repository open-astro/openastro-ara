### Fixed (mount)

- **A mount that may have lost power is no longer trusted at its false Home** (#2070): a mount without absolute encoders (the iOptron HAE29C) wakes reporting Home and Dec 90 wherever it points. Ara now marks the position unverified when the mount reports Home right after it connects, when its position jumped while it was disconnected, or when it starts reporting Home or jumps in Dec with nothing commanded. This applies to every mount. While unverified, GoTo, Park, the meridian flip and Polar Align are refused, and Home is allowed (see #2173) (409 `mount-position-unverified`, or a failed instruction in a plan); Sync, the direction pad, Stop, tracking and Unpark still work. The Mount window and the Settings mount panel say so, and offer "The mount IS at Home" (with a question first) once the mount reports Home, after its own zero search. The flag survives a reconnect and a daemon restart; that confirmation clears it, and after an unconfirmed stop a Home seen to run and end at Home clears it too (#2173). A safety or fault park that is refused this way stops the mount's motion and tracking instead and posts a Critical notification; a plan's Park that is refused stops tracking too. New route `POST /api/v1/equipment/telescope/confirm-home` and runtime fields `position_unverified` and `position_unverified_reason`.
