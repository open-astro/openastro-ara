### Breaking changes
- **Polar Align's stalled and slow turns have their own error reasons, and its turns are bounded tighter** (Polar Align, #2067, #2081): a turn whose reading makes no progress for 2 s (it was 10 s) now fails with `polar_align.error` reason `slew_stalled`, and one that takes more than twice its planned time at its speed (it was three times the slow travel plus 15 s, 45 s for a 10° turn) with `slew_timeout`; both were `slew_failed`, which now means only a refused motor command or a mount with no motor rate at or below the one asked for. A mount whose Dec reading moves more than 0.1° during an RA turn, or whose reported pier side changes, now ends the run (`dec_moved`, `pier_changed`). **After upgrading:** a client or script that matched `slew_failed` for a stall or a slow turn matches `slew_stalled` and `slew_timeout` too; a mount that now fails `dec_moved` needs its Dec clutch tightened.

### Fixed (polar alignment)
- **Polar Align stops a turn the moment the mount does something it was not told to** (Polar Align, #2067, #2081, #2083, #2048 ARA-11 to ARA-13): each RA turn now runs at the fastest rates the mount offers at or below 3°/s and 1°/s, never rounded up (an iOptron's 1°/s request ran at 1.07°/s; it now runs at 0.53°/s), and is bounded by twice its planned time at those rates (NINA's factor) and a 2 s stall, so a frozen reading costs about 4° of turn, not 21°. Every poll reads the position and the pier side: a Dec reading that moves, a pier side that changes, or an RA reading that jumps further than the motor can turn stops the motor at once (a jump never reverses it). After the motor stops, the turn waits for the mount to stand still on fresh readings, and a Stop meanwhile still sends AbortSlew; a mount that runs on more than 2° past the turn gets the confirmed stop at once (`turn_mismatch`, or `mount_did_not_stop` with the cut-power warning when that stop is not confirmed either). Each turn must end within max(2°, 10%) of its size with the Dec within 1° (`turn_mismatch`). The plate solves now cross-check the mount: two frames of a measurement whose field is turned about 180° mean the pier flipped (`pier_flip`), any other turn more than 10° from what the RA turn does (about 0°, or more on a roughly aligned mount near the pole) is `turn_mismatch`, and the run ends with tracking off before the next turn. A point of several frames (`frames_per_point`) is checked on its frames' mean angle.

### Added (polar alignment)
- **NINA's Dec-spread warning** (Polar Align, #2081): after the three points of a measurement, a spread of the mount's Dec reading over 2″ is logged and shown, in the full UI and Simple Mode, on the live readout (`dec_spread_arcsec` on the status and `polar_align.progress`) or on the Check's verdict: the measurement may be off; check the Dec clutch.
