### Fixed

- **The imaging defaults, the autofocus settings and the telescope optics load from the rig before they save** (#1807): Settings and Simple Mode. A Save that was never loaded, or that follows a failed load, now writes nothing and says why; a reply from the rig that lacks a setting, or names an unknown frame type, autofocus method or telescope type, counts as a failed load instead of filling in a default. A copy another device saved in between is refused and the panel offers Reload. A rig or profile change sends both back to not loaded. The offline cache fills them for planning only and is never saved, and once the rig connects its own copy replaces the cached one. In Simple Mode the cooler target, Autofocus during plans, Keep focus sharp, the AF filter and the autofocus exposure say "Not loaded" with Retry until the rig answers; a change the rig does not save is refused with a message and goes back to the rig's value, a change refused because another device saved first shows that device's value, and a cooler target the rig did not save no longer moves a running cooler. After one failed load, the next change tries the load once more instead of refusing every change until Retry. Before the profile loads, the cooler's On sends no target of its own. A run created while connected is no longer built from the app's built-in gain, offset, cooling and autofocus values when the rig's copy could not be loaded: it stops and says so. The dark library form fills the cooler target once the profile arrives from the rig. The telescope optics (Settings → Optics, the telescope name in File naming, and the aperture and focal length on Simple Mode's Rig page) follow the same rules: a connect or plate solve that fills them on the rig reloads them instead of being overwritten, Refresh from connected camera and the mount's telescope fill save only over the rig's copy, a reply missing a field fails the load, and the OAG guide focal length is never worked out from optics that did not load. Planning, Tonight's Sky and the field of view still read the cached copy, or no optics, when the rig's copy is not in.
