### Fixed (polar alignment)
- **Polar Align moves stop on time when the mount's position reads lag** (#2166): on an iOptron HAE29C through AlpacaBridge the mount's reported position trailed its motor by up to 2 seconds, so each Polar Align move ran on until the late reading caught up. The 0.3° Dec probe of Move to start position ran 1.9 seconds (about 1°, toward the pole) on the rig, and a 10° RA turn could overshoot by several degrees. Each motor move now also stops once the speeds it asked for, times how long they ran, add up to its distance, then measures where it really ended on fresh readings and tops up any shortfall over 0.1°. The wait for the mount to stand still after a move is 2.5 seconds (was 1.5) so a late reading is never taken for the final position.
- **Move to start position remembers which way the Dec motor turns, and reads a probe at the pole safely** (#2166): on the rig, a move from Home sent its 0.3° Dec probe over the pole, but the mount reported the pass over the pole about 6 seconds late, so the move read the probe as going away and went on over the pole until the pier-side check stopped it. The direction a probe learns is now saved for the mount and its driver (like the RA motor direction), and later moves go straight off the pole with no probe; a move that goes the wrong way forgets it, and **Test direction again** forgets it too. Without a saved direction, a probe from within about 1° of the pole waits until the mount's pier side and position stand still for 10 seconds, and if it went over the pole, the move comes back past the pole before it moves off it. A Dec move's pier-side log line no longer says "RA turn".
