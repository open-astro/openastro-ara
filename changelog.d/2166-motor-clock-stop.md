### Fixed (polar alignment)
- **Polar Align moves stop on time when the mount's position reads lag** (#2166): on an iOptron HAE29C through AlpacaBridge the mount's reported position trailed its motor by up to 2 seconds, so each Polar Align turn ran on until the late reading caught up: a 10° RA turn could overshoot by several degrees. Each motor move now also stops once the speeds it asked for, times how long they ran, add up to its distance, then measures where it really ended on fresh readings and tops up any shortfall over 0.1°. The wait for the mount to stand still after a move is 2.5 seconds (was 1.5) so a late reading is never taken for the final position. On the rig a position read could take more than half a second while the motor ran on, so the stop by the clock no longer waits for a read, and the fast part of a move also ends by the clock where the slow part starts.
