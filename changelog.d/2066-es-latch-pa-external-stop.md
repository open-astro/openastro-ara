### Breaking changes
- **The telescope endpoints refuse a GoTo, a pad move, Home and Park while Polar Align turns the mount** (#2066): `POST /equipment/telescope/slew` (not a sync), `/moveaxis` (a non-zero rate), `/home` and `/park` now answer 409 with the new Refusal cause `polar-align-moving-mount` while a Polar Align motor move runs or its stop is not confirmed yet. A sync, a pad stop and Stop Mount still go through. **After upgrading:** a client that drives the mount during Polar Align stops Polar Align (or sends Stop Mount) first; an older app shows the daemon's sentence for the new cause.
- **A Find Home accepted just before a stop is dropped** (#2066): `POST /equipment/telescope/home` waits for the guider to stop before it sends the homing. A Stop Mount, Emergency Stop or the meridian-flip watchdog that lands in that window now drops the homing (the 202 still stands), as it already dropped a Park or a GoTo. **After upgrading:** a client that sends Stop Mount right after Home sends Home again when it wants the homing.
- **A stop from outside now fails Polar Align as `slew_aborted_externally`** (#2067): Stop Mount during a Polar Align turn used to end the run with the `polar_align.error` reason `slew_failed`. **After upgrading:** a client or script that keys on `slew_failed` for a Stop Mount looks for `slew_aborted_externally` too.

### Fixed (polar alignment)
- **Emergency Stop latches before any of its steps, and any stop from outside ends Polar Align** (#2066, #2067, #2048): Emergency Stop now counts the stop in the mount before its event and its first rung, so a polar alignment between two turns can no longer start its next turn or switch tracking on while the dome or run-abort rung holds the ladder. Polar Align reads the mount's stop count once, when Start is called, and checks it before every turn and tracking switch-on, after every seed frame and live frame, and between the stages: a Stop Mount while it solves a frame no longer becomes the next turn's baseline and lets that turn start. Stop Mount, Emergency Stop, the meridian-flip watchdog, a Park and a Find Home each end the run with a sentence that says which. A Park or Find Home counts as such a stop, and the run's exit then sends no stop of its own (it would abort the park); a GoTo accepted just before a Park, whose move has not gone out yet, is dropped. The app waits 180 s (was 160 s) for Emergency Stop's report, past its longer ladder. Emergency Stop's polar-alignment step no longer waits for Polar Align to confirm the stop itself (up to 8 s, which could report the step as failed); the ladder stops the mount and the Polar Aligner again after it. The safety park and the equipment-fault park stop Polar Align and the Polar Aligner before they park, each bounded at 10 s. A Polar Aligner correction move checks for a stop from outside too, so a Stop Mount or Emergency Stop during a live frame is not answered with a Polar Aligner move.
