### Added (polar alignment)
- **Turn RA by hand in Polar Align, like NINA's manual mode** (#2083): the confirm step offers **Turn RA by hand** next to **Move the mount**. The run then sends the mount no motor move, no GoTo and no Find Home, never drives a Polar Aligner's motors, and never switches tracking on while it measures (at the end it gives back the tracking it found only when *Stop tracking when polar align ends* is off, as every run does); it asks for each turn ("Turn RA by hand about 10° toward the east, then let go"), shows how far the mount says RA has turned when you use the hand pad (with the clutch open it claims no angle, since the frames alone cannot give one when the mount is off the pole), and takes the next frame once the mount is still (with the clutch open, a few frames and at least 5 s after it asked, so a small nudge near the pole is not taken for the turn; a mount read that fails is not taken for an open clutch). A turn far past the two asked for ends the run (`hand_turn_too_far`). After a turn with the clutch open the mount's reading is wrong, so a motor Polar Align start is refused (`polar-align-clutch-turned`) until the mount is synced (Center does it) or reconnected; Turn RA by hand still works. Each Start is one measurement, its two turns the way the confirm step says, and a Check can be turned by hand from its own confirm step. The rest (the three-point fit, the knob readout, the Check's verdict) is unchanged, and a tube within 3° of the pole is refused as for the motor. Full UI and Simple Mode. A Start from a second device that picks the other way of turning while a run is going is refused (`polar-align-already-running`). API: `hand_turn` on `POST /equipment/polaralign/start`, `hand_turn_available` on the start plan, `hand_turn` and `hand_turn_wait` on the status, and the WebSocket event `polar_align.hand_turn`.
- **NINA's polar-align guidance** (#2083): the confirm step, the help and the user guide say where to point (a field fairly high toward the pole, at least about 15° above the horizon) and to keep the turns from crossing the meridian, so the tube cannot swing into the pier.

### Changed (polar alignment)
- **The azimuth readout says it is a true arc** (#2083): the azimuth error is labelled "true arc (cos alt)", and the Full UI shows NINA's figure beside it (larger by about 1/cos(latitude), 1.5× at 48°), so the two programs' numbers compare. A measurement that starts more than 2° from the pole now warns that the correction is less reliable, and over 10° to check the site, the start field, and that the mount turned only in RA, as NINA does.
