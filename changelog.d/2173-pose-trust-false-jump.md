### Fixed

- **A commanded move or one bad read no longer marks the mount unverified** (Mount, #2173): a position jump must now show on two readings at least 2 s apart, so one bad read (after an AlpacaBridge restart or a link fault) is ignored; a GoTo, park or Home explains every change while it runs, and a Dec that lands at the last GoTo's target is never a jump, so a resume's re-center slew from Home no longer locks the mount out for the night; a Dec past ±90° (Dec +90.71° at Home) is folded over the pole first.
- **Home is the way out of an unverified position** (Mount, #2173): Home is no longer refused while the position is unverified, and a home search that ends with the mount reporting Home trusts the position again by itself, with no eye check; the Mount panel and the Simple Mode pad keep Home on, the warning and the pre-flight row clear once the mount is trusted, and the Park refusal says Home is the safe way out. GoTo, Park and Polar Align stay refused until then.
