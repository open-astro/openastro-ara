### Fixed

- **A commanded move or one bad read no longer marks the mount unverified** (Mount, #2173): a position jump must now show on two readings at least 2 s apart, so one bad read (after an AlpacaBridge restart or a link fault) is ignored. A GoTo, park or Home explains every change while it runs, and a park or Home is followed until the mount stops, so a long park over slow Wi-Fi is no jump. A Dec that lands at the last GoTo's target is never a jump, so a resume's re-center slew from Home no longer locks the mount out for the night. A Dec past ±90° (Dec +90.71° at Home) is folded over the pole first. While a jump waits for its second reading, GoTo and Park wait for it too (a few seconds), and a command in that window no longer hides a real jump.
- **Home is the way out of an unverified position on the iOptron HAE29C** (Mount, #2173): with AlpacaBridge 6.0.0~beta3 or later, Home is no longer refused while the position is unverified. A home search that is seen to run and ends with the mount reporting Home trusts the position again by itself, with no eye check. The warning and the pre-flight row clear once the mount is trusted. On every other mount Home stays refused while unverified, because it goes by the mount's position model. The refusal says to run the zero search from the hand controller or app, then confirm Home. GoTo, Park and Polar Align stay refused until then.
