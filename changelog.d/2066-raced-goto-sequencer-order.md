### Fixed (mount)
- **A sequence slew or meridian flip sent after a Stop is no longer stopped by a GoTo that raced the Stop** (#2066): a GoTo whose command was on its way when Stop Mount or Emergency Stop landed is stopped again once its command returns, unless a newer move was sent after the stop. A sequence slew, Center or meridian flip counted as that newer move only once it reached the mount, not while it was still checking the mount's clock, position and coordinate system, so the GoTo's second stop could go out alongside it and stop it. It now counts from the moment the sequence asks for it, as a GoTo from the Mount panel does. If its own checks then refuse it, the raced GoTo is still stopped.
