### Fixed (mount)
- **A move sent after a Stop is no longer stopped by a GoTo that raced the Stop, and the raced GoTo is stopped at once** (#2066): a GoTo whose command was on its way when Stop Mount or Emergency Stop landed is stopped again once its command returns. That second stop no longer waits for a newer move that is still checking the mount's clock, position or coordinate system, or waiting for the guider or a meridian flip's pier-side change: it goes out at once, so the mount does not drive on to the stopped GoTo's target. The newer move (a GoTo, sequence slew, Center, meridian flip, park or Find Home) sends its own command only after that stop has reached the mount, so the stop cannot land after it and stop it.
