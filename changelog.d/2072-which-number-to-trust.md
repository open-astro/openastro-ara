### Breaking changes
- **Polar Align ends the run when the mount keeps tracking in the live readout** (Polar Align, #2072): a mount that refuses tracking off, accepts it but keeps tracking, starts tracking again during the live loop, is not connected when tracking off is sent, or reads as slewing during the live loop (a hand-pad slew; an iOptron reads tracking off while it slews) used to give a readout that took the mount's turn for knob motion; the run now fails with `tracking_not_off`. **After upgrading:** if Polar Align fails with "the mount kept tracking", check that the mount (or its driver) really turns tracking off, then start again.

### Fixed (Polar Align)
- **Polar Align confirms tracking is off before and during the live readout** (Polar Align, #2072): the live readout needs a camera fixed in alt/az, so the routine now waits until the mount reports tracking off, and not slewing, before the first live frame and checks both on every frame (a disconnected mount reads tracking off, so it confirms nothing and the run ends); before, the tracking-off write's answer was thrown away. Each live frame now logs its solved RA/Dec and the tracking state, and each seed logs the pointing the readout is anchored to.
- **The guiding residual names both causes when it disagrees with Polar Align** (guiding, #2072): "PA ≥ 17′ · disagrees with Polar Align" replaces "above Polar Align", and the tooltip says that either Dec backlash ate guide pulses or the mount shifted (or the alignment reference was wrong), and to run Polar Align again to tell which. The daemon publishes the call as `above_align` and the client follows it; for an older daemon and for stored rows (`GET …/guider/pa-residuals`, which carry no `above_align`) the client applies the same rule itself. The "PA residual from guiding" log line adds the Dec rate, the Dec pulses added back, the hour angle and Dec.

### Added (Polar Align)
- **Each number says what it measures** (Polar Align, #2072): the full UI's live readout and Verify result say they are the RA axis against the refracted pole from plate solves, with azimuth as the true arc on the sky (cos alt applied); the guiding residual's tooltip says it is a lower bound from Dec drift, taken later. When the camera points nearly due east or west of the pole, the full UI and Simple Mode warn that the readout reads the altitude bolt poorly (`readout_caution: "altitude_weak"`). The guiding residual's strings are now in English and pt-BR.
