### Changed (rig)
- **Your position limits now bind the whole night, not only your own GoTo** (#2140): a sequence's GoTo, a Center and a meridian flip to a target under the lowest altitude or past the counterweight limit are refused before the mount moves; the target is skipped by the block's error setting and a notice says why. The safety parks, the shutdown park and Emergency Stop are never held back by a limit, and Park and Home always work from any pointing.
- **A GoTo, Park or Home from the app waits for what holds the mount** (#2140): it is refused while a sequence, a Center, a polar alignment or End Night holds the mount, and the message names it. Force still lets a GoTo through a running sequence, and only that. Live View and the focus tools do not block a GoTo. Find Home on a parked mount is refused; unpark first.
