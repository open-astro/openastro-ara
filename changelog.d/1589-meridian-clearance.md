### Added

- **A clearance limit stops the mount before the camera can reach the pier or tripod** (meridian handling, #1589): turn on **Clearance limit** under Settings → Unattended running → When to stop and set the **Highest safe altitude** for this rig. While it is on, a GoTo from the app (409 `above-clearance-limit`, with `altitude_deg` and `limit_deg`; `force` does not bypass it), a plan's slew, a Center step and a meridian flip to a target at or above that altitude are refused and nothing moves; when the mount tracks up to it, Ara stops tracking and guiding, pauses the run and sends a critical alert saying why, and never slews or parks the mount on its own. It is off on new and upgraded profiles (`clearance_limit_enabled` false, `clearance_max_altitude_deg` 80), so nothing changes until you turn it on. One value per profile: it does not follow the rotator angle. Simple Mode's Mount window shows it. A safety PUT with an altitude not above 0° and under 90° is refused (400).
