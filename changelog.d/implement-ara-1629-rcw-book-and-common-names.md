### Added (sky album books, common names)

- The Sky Album has a third Book, RCW: the 181 southern H-alpha emission regions of Rodgers, Campbell and Whiteoak (1960), with Gum numbers as aliases so a frame saved as "Gum 15" fills its Card. An object in two Books is a Card in each, so the RCW count is the true count: M8, M16, M17, M20, C 69, C 92 and C 100 are RCW Cards too, carrying their Messier or Caldwell picture. Search, the Catalogs overlay and Tonight's Sky know RCW too; Cards show the nebula Placeholder until pictures arrive (#1629).
- Known objects show a common name beside their designation in Search and the plan, as `NGC 246 (Skull Nebula)`, and the name finds the object. A curated list is laid over the catalogs and never replaces a name the catalog has (#1720).
