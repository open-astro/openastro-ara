### Fixed (tests)
- **Polar Align turns on the fake GEM mount land the same on a loaded runner** (#2064): the fine-seed and repeated-start tests ran the fake mount on the wall clock with a 0.6° landing tolerance, which one poll late by about 60 ms breaks. The fake can now take a fixed time per fresh driver read on a manual clock (`FreshReadTakes`), so the mount turns a fixed amount per poll and those tests land within one read's travel whatever the load. `AbortIgnored` no longer uses up its count on an abort sent to a still mount, which could leave a scenario's real abort honoured while the test believed it ignored.
