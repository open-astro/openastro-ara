### Added
- **The app shows and drives the Polar Corrector** (#1971): Simple Mode's Polar align Screen gets a Polar Corrector block between Setup and Error with the test move, each correction (axis, amount, east/west or up/down), the settle, the measurement and the converged sentence, the device's position from its zero, Stop motors, Home and Resume, and a plain sentence for every stop cause; the hand-turn arrows dim and Verify and Done step aside while the motors work, and the status card gets a Polar Corrector line. Advanced Mode's Polar Align panel shows the same block. Settings › Devices shows the detected Polar Corrector read-only, with Turned by (Motors / By hand) under it.
