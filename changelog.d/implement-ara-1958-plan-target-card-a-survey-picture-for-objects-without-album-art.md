### Added (Simple Mode)

- **A survey picture for targets without Album Art** (#1958): the Plan target card and the Sky Map pick card show the DSS2 cutout the Sky Atlas preview uses (same source, same disk cache) when the target has no Album Art. A target with Art keeps its Art and fetches nothing, and a planet, moon, comet or minor planet gets none (it moves); a tap on the picture shows the survey credit; offline, or when the survey does not come, the cards look as before (the drawn placeholder on Plan, no picture on the Sky Map).
