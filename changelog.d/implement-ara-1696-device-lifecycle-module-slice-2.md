### Changed (device lifecycle)
- **Four more devices raise a fault when they stop answering** (#1696): the flat panel, observing conditions, switch and safety monitor now raise a fault when they stop answering or their essential read keeps failing, as the rotator and dome already did; a safety monitor whose first read or first connection check fails goes to Error and reads as not safe. All six peripherals (those four plus filter wheel and focuser) share one connect, probe and disconnect module, and a device you disconnected yourself is left alone by recovery and the reconnector.
