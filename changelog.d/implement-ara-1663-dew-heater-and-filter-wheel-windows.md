### Added (device windows)
- **A Dew heater row in the Camera window** (#1663): a camera with its own anti-dew heater (ZWO, ToupTek family, Player One) gets a Dew heater row in Simple Mode's Camera window: On / Off for an on/off heater, a slider with its level for one with power levels. The rig pairs the heater with the main camera by the serial number both report, never by name or order and never with the guide camera, and the row shows only while the heater's switch is connected. The camera status gains an optional `dew_heater` (`device_id`, `port_id`).
- **The Filter wheel window is the wheel itself** (#1676): the filter in place big on the left with its slot under it, and a wheel of glass cells on the right, each tinted the colour of that filter's glass, with a blue mark where the light passes. Tap a cell to change filter and the wheel turns the short way to it (it jumps with reduced motion). A wheel with more than 8 slots shows a bigger wheel with the name in the hub. The plan-running question and the manual swap work as before.
