A reply from the rig that lacks a setting or names an unknown Loop/Single mode counts as a failed load. Each change saves the rig's copy with only that change, with the version the app loaded: a copy another device saved in between is refused: Settings offers Reload, and the Imaging tab and Simple Mode show the rig's copy again so the next change saves, and a change the rig does not save goes back to the rig's value. A rig or profile change sends the settings back to not loaded. While they are not loaded, Done and Check wait (no tolerance is made up to judge the error against), Check names no turn, the Polar Aligner block names no tolerance, and Simple Mode's Polar align chip shows no setup.
