### Fixed (rig)
- **Emergency Stop, the safety park, the fault park and End Night now end a Center** (#2142): a Center started outside a sequence could slew the mount again after any of these stops, because none of them knew about it. Now every stop asks the one Rig Occupancy register, which ends each running routine (polar alignment and Center) through its own stop and waits, within a bound, for that stop to return. A routine whose stop does not return in time, or fails, is named in Emergency Stop's result ("stop_centering FAILED") and in the log.
