### Changed

- **The app keeps no built-in values for the settings your rig holds** (#1807): every profile section the rig owns (site, optics, imaging defaults, camera electronics, autofocus, plate solving, unguided, unattended running, guider, filter wheel policy, auto-connect, file names, notifications, diagnostics mode) is read strictly: a reply from the rig, or an offline cache entry, that lacks a setting counts as not loaded instead of being filled with the app's old default, and nothing is saved from it. Settings, Simple Mode and the planning screens show "Not loaded" (or "—") with Retry where they showed those defaults: Simple Mode's plan has no rows and Add to queue says why until the imaging defaults arrive; its guider steppers and gain show "—", and the aggressiveness sliders and the Rig page's guide focal length say not loaded; Equipment's weather, flat panel and safety monitor rows, the OAG guide focal length and the matching-flats frame count wait for the rig's copy. A run is not built, online or offline, while the imaging defaults or autofocus settings are not loaded; a guided run also needs the guider settings, and a run with no guider the unguided settings. Tonight's Sky, the planetarium and the sub-exposure advice plan with no field of view or sub figure, or no site, rather than invented ones; the Optimal Sub calculator opens with those inputs unset. An offline cache written by an older app that lacks a field leaves that section not loaded until the app reconnects and refreshes the cache; the other sections still plan from it. An older daemon with no unguided section must be updated before a rig with no guider can run: the app's unguided read now fails on its 404 instead of using the app's defaults, and no such defaults are cached. The setup wizard writes a cleared ASTAP or star database path as blank (''), and the daemon maps a blank path to its bundled ASTAP or database when that is installed.
