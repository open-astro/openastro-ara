### Added

- **The camera's framing box on the Simple Mode sky**, Simple Mode, #1899. A Framing button on the sky switches the box on for the picked Target, with the angle dial and a degrees stepper (east of north) and a Snap to catalogue angle button for galaxies and nebulae that have one; the controls are sized for touch and a finger drag turns the dial. On an upright phone the card docks as a strip under the status card, clear of the view centre where the box sits. Add to queue then queues the Target framed at that angle. With no camera optics set, the card says so in place of the box. Picking another Target sets Framing back to Off and the angle to 0 degrees; picking the same Target keeps both. The Full UI keeps its box and angle across targets.
