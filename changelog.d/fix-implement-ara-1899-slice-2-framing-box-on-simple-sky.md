### Added

- **The camera's framing box on the Simple Mode sky**, Simple Mode, #1899. A Framing button on the sky switches the box on for the picked Target, with the angle dial and a degrees stepper (east of north) and Add to queue. Picking another Target sets Framing back to Off and the angle to 0 degrees; picking the same Target keeps both. The Full UI keeps its box and angle across targets.
