### Added (Mount)

- **Check an unverified mount position with a plate solve** (Mount, #2173): when the mount's position is unverified, the Mount window and Simple Mode offer "Check position with a plate solve". It takes one short exposure with the main camera (the guide camera only when there is no main camera), solves it with ASTAP and compares it with the mount, without moving the mount at all. When the sky and the pier side agree, the position is trusted again, whatever made it unverified. When the sky is somewhere else, it shows where the mount reads and where the sky is, and offers one tap to Sync; after the Sync it checks again with a second solve and trusts the position only when that agrees too. A pier side that does not match (checked from the hour angle and from the camera's angle at the last trusted solve) is never trusted and gets no Sync: Home stays the way out. A failed solve, a busy camera or no camera leaves the position unverified.
