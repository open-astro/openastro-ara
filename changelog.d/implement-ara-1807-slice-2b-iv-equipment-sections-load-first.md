### Fixed

- **The equipment auto-connect switches, the cooling fan and the dome settings load from the rig before they save** (#1807): Settings. An auto-connect switch, a fan choice or a dome geometry edit that was never loaded, or that follows a failed load, now writes nothing and says why; a reply from the rig that lacks a device type, a fan key or a dome setting counts as a failed load instead of filling in a default. A copy another device saved in between is refused and the panel offers Reload; any other failed save puts an auto-connect switch back to the rig's value. With no rig, or before they load, the auto-connect switches read off, cannot be changed and say why (with Retry after a failed load), and the dome panel and the cooling fan picker say their settings are not loaded (with Retry when a rig is connected). Switching the dome to follow the mount, or the daemon turning following off, reloads the dome settings so the next geometry edit is not refused.
