### Fixed

- **The equipment auto-connect switches, the cooling fan and the dome settings load from the rig before they save** (#1807): Settings. An auto-connect switch, a fan choice or a dome geometry edit that was never loaded, or that follows a failed load, now writes nothing and says why; a reply from the rig that lacks a device type, a fan key or a dome setting counts as a failed load instead of filling in a default. A copy another device saved in between is refused and the panel offers Reload. With no rig, or before they load, the auto-connect switches read off and cannot be changed, the dome panel says its settings are not loaded, and the cooling fan shows no choice. Switching the dome to follow the mount, or the daemon turning following off, reloads the dome settings so the next geometry edit is not refused.
