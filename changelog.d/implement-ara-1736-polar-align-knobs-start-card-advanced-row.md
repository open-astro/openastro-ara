### Added (polar align)
- **Advanced row on the Polar Align Start card** (#1736): a collapsed Advanced row under the Start buttons opens the routine's four knobs (send the mount Home first, the turn, the Verify step and the rough exposure) in two columns, one on a phone. Each edit saves at once and shows in Settings › Imaging › Polar align too; a value out of range is refused in the field with its range, and a failed save puts the field back to the rig's value. The knobs stay hidden until the rig's settings load, and a failed load shows Retry.
- **One help entry per Polar align setting** (#1736): the shared "Polar align settings" entry is replaced by one entry per setting, and "Polar alignment in three steps" links to them. Send the mount Home first says that off takes the mount's current pointing as Home, which you set right before each stage.
