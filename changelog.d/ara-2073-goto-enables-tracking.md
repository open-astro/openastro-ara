### Breaking changes

- **A GoTo to a target below the pole near the lower meridian is refused** (#2073): `POST /api/v1/equipment/telescope/slew` now answers 409 with `reason` `counterweight-limit` on a German equatorial mount for a target more than 10 h from the meridian (within 2 h of the lower meridian, under the pole), where the counterweight would be more than 60° from hanging down. Before, such a GoTo was sent to the mount. An alt-az or fork mount (ASCOM `AlignmentMode` alt-az or polar) is not checked; a mount that does not report its type is checked. **After upgrading:** a GoTo to a low circumpolar target in that band is refused until it rises; pick another target or wait. An app older than this daemon shows the daemon's English sentence.

### Fixed (GoTo)

- **A GoTo from the Mount panel turns tracking on first** (#2073): the daemon now enables tracking right before it sends the GoTo, as Park and sequence GoTos already did, so a mount that is stopped or at Home (for example right after Find Home) no longer refuses it with "SlewToCoordinatesAsync requires Tracking to be true". A GoTo, Park, Unpark or Find Home that the mount refuses now has the driver's reason in the log line itself, not only in the exception. A Stop Mount or emergency stop that lands while the GoTo is still waiting to be sent (for the guider to stop, or for the tracking write) drops the GoTo, so it can no longer turn tracking back on and move the mount after the stop. A Park from the Mount panel (or a safety or fault park) is dropped the same way when a stop lands before it is sent.
- **A GoTo that would put the counterweight up is refused before the mount moves** (#2073): turning tracking on removed the accidental barrier that stopped two counterweight-up GoTos at hour angle −11.1 h on 2026-11-19, so a REST GoTo below the pole past #2048's 60° counterweight angle is refused with `counterweight-limit` (hour angle, counterweight angle and limit), worded by the app in English and pt-BR. The horizon check runs first, a sync is not checked, `force` does not bypass it, a profile with no site lets the GoTo through, and only a German equatorial mount (or one that does not report its `AlignmentMode`) is checked, so alt-az and fork mounts keep targets near the lower meridian such as Polaris. The limit is fixed at 60° until #2048 adds its setting.
