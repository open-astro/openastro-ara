### Breaking changes
- **Polar Align starts from the mount's Home position, with no checklist and nothing to confirm** (Polar Align): send the mount Home (tube at the pole, counterweights down), then tap Start. A mount that can find Home and is not there is refused with the new Refusal cause `polar-align-not-home`; a mount that cannot find Home is not asked, so set it to its home position yourself. A start at the pole is fine now. Gone, in the rig and the app: the pre-flight list (`GET /equipment/polaralign/preflight`), the clearance question and its saved answer (`polar_align.mount_clearance`), the confirm step and its plan (`GET /equipment/polaralign/start-plan`, the start body's `confirmed`, `direction`, `new_origin` and `direction_test`), the 0.5° motor-direction test and its saved result (`polar_align_motor_direction`, `GET` and `POST …/polaralign/motor-direction`), the limit on how far RA may be from where Polar Align first started, **Rough first** and the `coarse` stage, and `POST /equipment/polaralign/next`. The Refusal causes `polar-align-unconfirmed`, `polar-align-sweep-limit`, `polar-align-on-axis`, `polar-align-pointing-unknown`, `polar-align-pose-ambiguous`, `polar-align-clearance-not-confirmed`, `polar-align-already-running`, `polar-align-site-not-set`, `polar-align-alt-az-mount` and `polar-align-cannot-move-axis` are gone with them, and so are the `polar_align.error` reasons `slew_stalled`, `slew_timeout`, `dec_moved`, `pier_changed`, `pier_flip`, `turn_mismatch`, `turn_wrong_way`, `sweep_limit`, `pointing_unknown`, `pose_ambiguous`, `on_axis` and `mount_busy`, the status key `waiting_for_home` and `dec_spread_arcsec` everywhere. **After upgrading:** update the app with the rig, and send the mount Home before each Start.
- **Done finishes the whole job: the mount goes Home, the alignment is verified, and the mount goes Home again** (Polar Align): one button starts Polar Align and one button ends it. Tap Start with the mount at Home: Ara measures (three frames, two short RA turns), then shows the live readout while you turn the knobs. Tap Done: Ara stops the mount, sends it Home, measures again to verify what you did, sends the mount Home once more and shows the verdict beside your last readout, with tracking off. You can then start imaging, or tap Start to adjust again. Stop works at any moment, also while the mount is on its way Home, and leaves the mount where it is. A mount that cannot find Home by itself makes no Home trip: the verify measurement turns it back to where the run started. If the mount refuses Find Home or does not finish it, the run ends with the new reason `home_failed` and the mount is stopped. There is no separate Check any more: the start request has no `stage` (a body is ignored), `POST /equipment/polaralign/complete` answers at once and no longer ends the run, the status has the new state `homing`, `stage` reads `align` or `verify` (was `fine` or `check`), and the `polar_align.stage` event carries `check_result` once the verdict is known. The settings `polar_align.check_step_deg` (the verify measurement turns by `seed_rotation_deg`) and `polar_align.stop_tracking_when_done` (every run ends with tracking off) are gone; a saved profile that still has them loads as before. **After upgrading:** a script that started a check with `stage: check`, or waited for `stopped` after `complete`, must start a run, call `complete`, and wait for the state `checked` (or the `polar_align.checked` event) instead.

### Fixed (polar alignment)
- **Polar Align no longer ends a run over the mount's readings** (Polar Align): on an iOptron HAE29C over Wi-Fi, whose replies stall for 1 to 4 seconds, every run was ended by one of Polar Align's own checks of the mount's readings (a stale reading, a pier side that "changed" near the pole, a turn that "did not match"). A turn is now timed: the RA motor runs at the fastest speed the mount offers at or below 3°/s, never faster, for as long as the turn takes at that speed, then stops, and the mount gets 2.5 s to settle. Nothing reads the mount's position during or after a turn. The plate solves say what the mount did: when the three frames show the same field (two of them closer than 20″: the mount did not turn, or the camera looks straight down the RA axis), or a turn the frames show is under 0.3 or over 3 times the turn the motor was told (a slipping clutch, a stalled motor), the run ends with `axis_fit_failed` and says which; a turn that overran because its stop was slow to arrive still measures. A camera only a fraction of a degree off the RA axis now measures (the 1° minimum is gone), and a mount whose driver turns RA the other way than asked measures the same. Stop, Stop Mount, Emergency Stop and a park still stop the motor at once, and every end of a run still stops the mount and confirms it. That confirmation now counts a reading of the mount by when the mount took it, not by how long it took to arrive, so a mount whose replies stall no longer ends a good run with `mount_did_not_stop`; and a turn no longer renews the motor's lease near its end, so the stop is never held up behind a renewal.
