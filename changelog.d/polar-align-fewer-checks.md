### Breaking changes
- **Polar Align starts from the mount's Home position, with no checklist and nothing to confirm** (Polar Align): send the mount Home (tube at the pole, counterweights down), then tap Start. A mount that can find Home and is not there is refused with the new Refusal cause `polar-align-not-home`; a mount that cannot find Home is not asked, so set it to its home position yourself. A start at the pole is fine now. Gone, in the rig and the app: the pre-flight list (`GET /equipment/polaralign/preflight`), the clearance question and its saved answer (`polar_align.mount_clearance`), the confirm step and its plan (`GET /equipment/polaralign/start-plan`, the start body's `confirmed`, `direction`, `new_origin` and `direction_test`), the 0.5° motor-direction test and its saved result (`polar_align_motor_direction`, `GET` and `POST …/polaralign/motor-direction`), the limit on how far RA may be from where Polar Align first started, **Rough first** and the `coarse` stage, and `POST /equipment/polaralign/next`. The Refusal causes `polar-align-unconfirmed`, `polar-align-sweep-limit`, `polar-align-on-axis`, `polar-align-pointing-unknown`, `polar-align-pose-ambiguous`, `polar-align-clearance-not-confirmed`, `polar-align-already-running`, `polar-align-site-not-set`, `polar-align-alt-az-mount` and `polar-align-cannot-move-axis` are gone with them, and so are the `polar_align.error` reasons `slew_stalled`, `slew_timeout`, `dec_moved`, `pier_changed`, `pier_flip`, `turn_mismatch`, `turn_wrong_way`, `sweep_limit`, `pointing_unknown`, `pose_ambiguous`, `on_axis` and `mount_busy`, the status key `waiting_for_home` and `dec_spread_arcsec` everywhere. **After upgrading:** update the app with the rig. Send the mount Home before each Start and each Check; a Check from the adjust screen is refused on a mount that can find Home until you tap Done and send it Home. A script starts a run with `POST /equipment/polaralign/start` and at most a `stage` (`fine` or `check`).

### Fixed (polar alignment)
- **Polar Align no longer ends a run over the mount's readings** (Polar Align): on an iOptron HAE29C over Wi-Fi, whose replies stall for 1 to 4 seconds, every run was ended by one of Polar Align's own checks of the mount's readings (a stale reading, a pier side that "changed" near the pole, a turn that "did not match"). A turn is now timed: the RA motor runs at the fastest speed the mount offers at or below 3°/s, never faster, for as long as the turn takes at that speed, then stops, and the mount gets 2.5 s to settle. Nothing reads the mount's position during or after a turn. The plate solves say what the mount did: when the three frames show the same field (two of them closer than 20″: the mount did not turn, or the camera looks straight down the RA axis), or a turn the frames show is more than 5° from the turn the motor was told (a slipping clutch, a stalled motor), the run ends with `axis_fit_failed` and says which. A camera only a fraction of a degree off the RA axis now measures (the 1° minimum is gone), and a mount whose driver turns RA the other way than asked measures the same. Stop, Stop Mount, Emergency Stop and a park still stop the motor at once, and every end of a run still stops the mount and confirms it.
