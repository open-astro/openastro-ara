### Changed (Simple Mode Plan)

- **Plan shows the picked target as a card** (#1875): the target you pick, from Tonight's Best, Recent, the search or the sky, is a card at the top of the Plan window with its name, its altitude now and the hours it stays above 30° before dawn ("3.5 h left above 30°", or "Not above 30° tonight"); its list card is tinted too.
- **Tonight's Best is ranked for the rest of tonight** (#1875): the list orders targets by the hours they stay above 30° from now to dawn, framing as the tie-break, and ranks again every 5 minutes while Plan is open.
- **Plan lists the last 5 targets you imaged or searched** (#1875): a Recent list, newest first, merges the Stats targets with your saved targets; one that never rises above 30° tonight stays, greyed, marked "not up tonight".
- **The frames line warns when frames don't fit** (#1875): "Fits N of them before 30°/dawn" shows when the plan has more frames than the hours left allow; the typed count stays.
