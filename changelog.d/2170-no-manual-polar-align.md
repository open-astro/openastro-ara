### Changed (polar alignment)
- **Polar Align always turns RA with the mount's motor: there is no manual mode** (#2170): the hand-turn mode is gone before any release shipped it, as Ara is built to automate a computer-controlled equatorial mount. The confirm step offers Cancel and Move the mount only, and no screen asks you to turn RA by hand. A mount whose clearance is not confirmed keeps Start and Check off until you confirm it, and a mount that cannot turn its RA axis by motor speed is refused (`polar-align-cannot-move-axis`) with no fallback. API: the `hand_turn` field on `POST /equipment/polaralign/start` and the `hand_turn` query on `start-plan` and `preflight` are gone (a request that still sends them is a plain motor start), with `hand_turn_available`, `hand_turn_wait`, the `polar_align.hand_turn` event, the `hand_turn_too_far` reason and the `polar-align-clutch-turned` refusal.
