### Added

- **Mount limits in Simple Mode** (#2044): the Mount window (also Settings › Mount, and on a phone) has a Limits group under its rows and direction pad. Lowest altitude sets the profile's flat horizon from 0° to 60° in 5° steps (read-only, with where to change it, when a measured horizon is in use). At the meridian (flip Auto) sets when the flip runs, from 30 min before the meridian to 30 min after it. Stop before meridian (flip Off) is Off or 5 to 30 min. A sky arc draws the lowest altitude. Each tap saves at once with a pill; a failed save puts the old value back. The highest-altitude row waits for its server field (#1589).
- **The daemon stops tracking before the meridian when the flip is off** (#2044): with Stop before meridian set, `MeridianStopWatch` stops tracking and pauses the running plan that many minutes before the meridian, once per approach, and publishes `telescope.limit_reached`, which Simple Mode shows as an error pill and under the paused plan. It runs with or without a plan and never slews or parks.
- **Meridian flip window on the wire** (#2044): the safety policies gain `minutes_after_meridian` (default 5), `max_minutes_after_meridian` (default 10) and `stop_before_meridian_min` (default null, off). The first two are applied to the flip settings with `meridian_pause_min`, so an older profile flips as before. The full UI edits all three in Settings › Unattended running › When to stop, beside Stop before meridian (min): Flip after the meridian (min), Latest flip after the meridian (min) and Stop before meridian, flip off (min).
