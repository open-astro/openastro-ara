### Added (Simple Mode)

- **A Target framed in the planetarium turns to the framed angle when queued from Simple Mode** (#1899): Add to queue and Start now send Center and Rotate at the dialed angle on the framing centre (0° too), and editing the Target or pressing Undo after removing it keeps that framing. A Target with no framing, or a framing named for another Target, keeps the plain centred go-to.

### Fixed (Simple Mode)

- **Undo after removing a Target keeps its After target flats** (#1899): the put-back block had dropped them.
