### Breaking changes
- **An unconfirmed Polar Align stop, or a mount reconnect during Polar Align, blocks GoTo, Park and a new polar alignment** (#2066, #2065, #2173): a polar alignment that ends with "cut the mount's power" (`mount_did_not_stop`), or whose mount disconnected and connected again while it was open, now marks the mount's position unverified (`position_unverified_reason` `stop_unconfirmed`). GoTo, Park, a new polar alignment and a sequence's GoTo, Park, Find Home and flip are refused (409 `mount-position-unverified`) as after a power loss, across reconnects and daemon restarts. The user's Home still runs on a mount that can find Home (#2173). **After upgrading:** when the mount window says the position is unverified, press Home. After an unconfirmed stop on a mount that kept its power, a Home Ara sees move and end at Home clears it by itself; after a power loss, a reset or a jump, confirm by eye that the mount is at Home, then confirm Home in the mount window (`POST /equipment/telescope/confirm-home`). A mount with no Home in Ara: run its zero search from its hand controller or app, then confirm Home. Stop, the direction pad and tracking still work meanwhile. Polar Align's start confirmation says the position is unverified too, before it checks how close the scope points to the pole, so a mount that wakes up at Home after a power cycle is not told to point 2–5° from the pole.

### Fixed (polar alignment)
- **Polar Align says which stop ended it, in your language** (#2107, #2066): when the equipment-fault reaction or the safety reaction could not park the mount and stopped it instead, a polar alignment it ended said "Stop Mount", although nobody pressed it; it now names that reaction. The `polar_align.error` event carries the stop's name (`stopped_by`: Stop Mount, Emergency Stop, the meridian-flip watchdog, a park, a Find Home, the direction pad, the fault reaction or the safety reaction), and the app words the end from it in English or Brazilian Portuguese (the direction pad's sentence was English only), with the "cut the mount's power" warning when Ara could not confirm the stop afterwards (`stop_unconfirmed`). A routine's motor renewal refused after a stop, and a Park or Find Home dropped because a stop came after it (a second park in flight among them), now name that stop in their message and log line. The safety and fault reactions share one set of quiet stops before their park, so the two parks stay in step.
- **A "could not confirm the mount stopped" found late is no longer lost** (#2066): when a polar alignment's own exit could not confirm its stop only after Stop had already reported the run as stopped, the finding could go unreported; it is now reported once, and the run shows as failed.
