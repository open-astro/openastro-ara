### Changed (equipment)
- **The Polar Corrector is now the Polar Aligner** (#1971): the name follows ASCOM's draft aligner interface. This only matters to a rig running a build made from master on 2026-11-16 or 2026-11-17, the two days the old names existed; no release had them. **Update the daemon and the app together.** An app from those days asks the old routes, shows "no device" and cannot stop or resume the motors on a newer daemon. Old to new: `/equipment/polarcorrector` to `/equipment/polaraligner`; `/equipment/polaralign/corrector/stop|resume` to `/equipment/polaralign/correction/stop|resume`; status object `corrector` to `correction`; event `polar_align.corrector` to `polar_align.correction`; Emergency Stop rung `stop_polar_corrector` to `stop_polar_aligner`; profile field `polar_corrector_auto` to `polar_aligner_auto`. A profile saved with `polar_corrector_auto: false` reads as on again: set "Turned by" to "By hand" once more.
