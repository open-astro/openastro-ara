### Added (Simple Mode)

- **Tonight's Best screen** (#1900): the ranked Targets for the rest of tonight as a page of their own, opened from the left rail's Best item at every width, the phone deck's seventh word Best, or See all on Plan's Tonight's Best row. Each row shows the type, framing fit, best window and transit, highest altitude and altitude now ("below the horizon" when it is under the horizon), the Moon distance only where the Moon hurts, and In Queue and Collected markers as a word plus a glyph. Sort by Score, Transit or Altitude now; filter by type, Up now, Peak altitude and Fits my field (disabled with a reason until a camera and telescope give a field); Clear filters resets the four filters and keeps Sort. The list is the one the plan sheet's four tiles show the top of, re-ranked on each sky clock tick or Refresh. A row tap shows the Target on the sky; Image this opens Plan's sheet on it, or adds it to the running plan's Queue with a pill. Sort and filters are kept on this device. Strings in English and Brazilian Portuguese.
