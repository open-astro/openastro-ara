### Added (polar alignment)
- **Move to start position** (#2166): when Polar Align refuses to start because the scope points within 3° of the pole, the pre-flight list (Full UI and Simple Mode) now shows a **Move to start position** button on that row. After you confirm on the motion dialog, Polar Align turns the Dec axis alone until the scope points about 4° from the pole: no GoTo, the RA axis and the counterweights stay where they are. It first moves Dec 0.3° to learn which way the motor turns, and if that went toward the pole (or over it, from Home) it stops, settles and then moves the other way, ending on the side of the pier it started on. The move has the guards of every Polar Align turn (a reading every 250 ms, a 2 s stall check, twice its planned time, Stop, Stop Mount and Emergency Stop, a pier side that changes, and the RA reading may not move) and shows its progress with a Stop button that stays until the move ends. The button is off, with the reason, while the mount is parked, its position is unverified, its clearance is not confirmed for the move, or it cannot turn its Dec axis by motor speed. Nothing moves before you tap, and pointing by hand works as before. API: `POST /api/v1/equipment/polaralign/move-to-start`, the pre-flight list's `move_to_start` offer, and the refusals `polar-align-run-active`, `polar-align-cannot-move-dec` and `polar-align-move-to-start-not-needed`.
