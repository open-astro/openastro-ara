### Added (polar alignment)
- **Move to start position** (#2166): when Polar Align refuses to start because the scope points within 3° of the pole, the pre-flight list (Full UI and Simple Mode) now shows a **Move to start position** button on that row. After you confirm on the motion dialog, Polar Align turns the Dec axis alone until the scope points about 4° from the pole: no GoTo, the RA axis and the counterweights stay where they are. The first time on a mount it moves Dec 0.3° to learn which way the Dec motor turns and saves that for the mount and the side of the pier, so later moves go straight off the pole; a small move toward the pole is stopped and settled before a new move goes the other way. From Home or within half a degree of the pole the first small move only takes the scope off the pole, onto whichever side the mount picks, and the move ends on that side (#2189). The move has the guards of every Polar Align turn (a reading every 250 ms, a 2 s stall check, twice its planned time, Stop, Stop Mount and Emergency Stop, a pier side that changes, and the RA reading may not move) and shows its progress with a Stop button that stays until the move ends, also after a tab or mode switch. A mount whose Dec motor is too slow to finish within 5 minutes is refused before anything moves. The button is off, with the reason, while the mount is parked, the site is not set, its position is unverified, its clearance is not confirmed, it is an alt-az mount, or it cannot turn its Dec axis by motor speed. Nothing moves before you tap and confirm, and the rig itself checks that confirmation, so no other app or script can start the move without it. Pointing the scope yourself works as before. API: `POST /api/v1/equipment/polaralign/move-to-start`, the pre-flight list's `move_to_start` offer, its `{confirmed, target_off_pole_deg, reach_deg}` body, and the refusals `polar-align-run-active`, `polar-align-cannot-move-dec`, `polar-align-move-to-start-not-needed` and `polar-align-move-not-confirmed`.
