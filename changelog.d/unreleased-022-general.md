### Added
- **Polar alignment left after Align, measured from guiding (#1311).** No extra setup step: each guided run's first 5 minutes of clean guiding are fitted for Dec drift. The daemon adds every Dec correction back onto the star's offset (pulse length × the calibration's Dec rate) to rebuild the drift the guider absorbed, then converts it to a polar alignment error (1″/min ≈ 3.8′). The Live tab's guiding strip shows "PA residual · measuring", then **PA ≥ 48″ · Excellent** with the Polar Align card's bands. The tooltip has the uncertainty, the drift, the hour angle and tonight's Polar Align result. The figure is a lower bound, because one hour angle sees one component of the error. Unlike PHD2's Guiding Assistant, it is not divided by cos(Dec): Dec drift does not depend on the star's Dec, and that factor overstates the error by 41 % at Dec 45°. Dithers and settling are left out. Lock-position shift (comet tracking) or an adaptive-optics unit makes it unavailable, and a noisy fit is flagged instead of rated. Results are logged with the imaging session, and the Image Library shows them in the session header. New WS event `guider.pa_residual`, `runtime.pa_residual` on `GET /api/v1/equipment/guider`, and `GET /api/v1/equipment/guider/pa-residuals?sessionId=`.
- **Bahtinov mask focusing (#1299).** Setup → Smart Focus → Main telescope gains an Autofocus | Bahtinov mask picker; it opens on Bahtinov when no focuser is connected. Fit the mask, point at a bright star and press Start: each frame the daemon finds the brightest star, fits the three spikes and reports how far the middle spike sits from the centre of the X, signed, with an estimate in µm and whether that is inside the critical focus zone (from the focal ratio and pixel size; half a pixel without them). The card draws the fitted lines over the star, shows the offset as a big number with a trend, and gives advice relative to your last move ("Keep going — about half your last move again", "You passed focus"). Finish asks you to remove the mask and marks the scope focused when the last reading was in the zone. Autofocus runs and sequence starts are refused (409 `bahtinov_focus_active`) while the readout runs. New endpoints under `/api/v1/bahtinov-focus` (`start`, `stop`, `state`, `frame`). Manual focusers get guidance only; driving a motorised focuser to null the offset is a follow-up.
- **The Run editor says what a loop does (#1298 review).** A container row used to show only "⟳ 1 ⚡ 2", the number of conditions and triggers, so a 56-frame Ha loop read as "loops once". Rows now read "× 56 · 5.2 min · ≈ 4.8 h", "AF every 23 · dither every 1" and "while above horizon". A selected Take Exposure says "Repeats 56× in Ha Imaging (≈ 4.8 h) · AF every 23 · dither every 1" under its one-frame description.
- **Delete in the Run toolbar removes the highlighted item.** With an instruction or target block selected, Delete removes just that item and offers Undo, like the row's trash icon. Only with nothing selected does the button read "Delete sequence" and delete the whole sequence after confirming. It used to always delete the whole sequence and every target in it.
- **Manual filter wheel (#1298).** Rigs with a filter drawer, slider or hand-turned wheel can pick "Manual filter wheel" from the filter-wheel chooser. It needs no driver and no network. Its filters are the names under Settings → Equipment → Filter wheel. When a run, the Imaging tab or a plate solve needs a different filter, Ara shows an "Install the X filter" prompt on every screen, posts a notification, shows it in the run band, and waits with no time limit until you press "X is in". Cancel swap fails that step instead, and its retries apply. The installed filter is remembered across daemon restarts, and `SwitchFilter`, focus offsets and per-filter autofocus resolve it as they do for a motorised wheel. New endpoints: `POST /api/v1/equipment/filterwheel/installed` and `/swap/cancel`. The filter wheel status gains `manual` and `runtime.pending_slot`, and a new `equipment.filter_wheel.manual_swap` WS event carries the prompt.
- **Sky photo cache housekeeping (#1143).** The planetarium's DSS2 tile cache caps itself at 512 MB (oldest-fetched tiles evicted first; the survey manifest, Allsky previews and order ≤ 3 tiles are never evicted), sweeps orphaned `.part-*` temp files at launch, keys the engine's `?v=` cache-buster so a survey re-release is fetched again, holds tile misses beyond six in-flight upstream fetches behind one connect deadline instead of one per miss, and carries the per-run token in its route path. Settings → Storage gains a "Sky photos (this computer)" row with the size and a Clean cache button.

### Fixed
- **Sequence frames record their filter.** A run's frames were saved with no filter for every wheel, motorised or manual: an exposure step names no filter (a separate Switch Filter step sets it), and the camera took the name only from the step. Captures that name no filter now take the wheel's installed filter, so the FITS `FILTER` header, the frame record and the `{filter}` filename token carry it. This also revives the HFR-drift autofocus trigger on rigs with a wheel, which compares frames taken on the current filter and so never saw any.
- **"Saved as an offline draft" while connected.** Creating an imaging run from Planning with nothing else watching the sequence API (the new coordinates dialog, or a Tonight's Sky row whose panel had just closed) let the client's HTTP connection be released mid-create, so the run silently became a local draft instead of landing on the daemon. The create now holds the connection for its whole duration.
