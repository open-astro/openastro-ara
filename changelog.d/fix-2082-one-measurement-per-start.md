### Breaking changes
- **Polar Align makes one measurement per Start, and Next is gone** (Polar Align, #2082): the automatic Rough → Fine → Check chain is gone. Start runs one measurement (the guide camera's, which used to be the second step) and then follows your knob moves until Done or Stop; it never moves on to another measurement by itself. `POST /equipment/polaralign/next` now always answers 409, and a start with no `stage` is the fine measurement, not the rough one. **After upgrading:** update the app with the rig. To check the result, tap **Check** (on the adjust screen, or after Done): it ends the adjust loop and, once you confirm, turns the mount 2 × the check step. A script that called `next` starts a check with `POST /equipment/polaralign/start` and `stage: "check"`, confirmed like any start.
- **Polar Align refuses a start within 3° of the pole** (Polar Align, #2082): it used to tilt the tube 5° off the pole with the Dec motor first. A start (and the confirm step's plan) with the scope within 3° of the pole is now refused with the new Refusal cause `polar-align-on-axis`, before anything moves. **After upgrading:** point the tube 2–5° from the pole, counterweights down, then start.

### Changed (polar alignment)
- **Check is its own confirmed start, inside the session's 30° limit** (Polar Align, #2082): Check (the old Verify) is a new measurement that goes through the confirm step and says how far it turns ("Check turns the mount 2 × 10°"), in both the full UI and Simple Mode. From the adjust screen it ends the live loop like Stop (the mount stopped and confirmed) once the check is confirmed; a refused check leaves the loop running. Every Start and Check turns the way its plan says, toward where Polar Align first started, so repeated Checks are refused once RA would go more than 30° from it. The check's *Last run said* figure, and its *Disagreement*, now compare with the readout of the last run that ended with one (the fine step's no longer leads into the check). That is the plan's proposal, open for Joey to overrule. The Start card's *Skip the rough step* and *Check only* are replaced by **Start** and **Check**; **Rough first (main camera)** is offered only after the guide camera could not solve the pole field. The stage strip and Simple Mode's stepper tick only a finished check, and the confirm step no longer shows the "later steps" line. Polar Align never moves the Dec axis now.
