### Breaking changes
- **Polar Align makes one measurement per Start, and Next is gone** (Polar Align, #2082): the automatic Rough → Fine → Check chain is gone. Start runs one measurement and then follows your knob moves until Done or Stop; it never moves on to another measurement by itself. `POST /equipment/polaralign/next` is removed. **After upgrading:** update the app with the rig. To check the result, tap **Check**: it turns the mount 2 × the check step and measures on its own. A script that called `next` starts a check with `POST /equipment/polaralign/start` and `stage: "check"`.

### Changed (polar alignment)
- **Check is its own start** (Polar Align, #2082): Check (the old Verify) is a new measurement that says how far it turns ("Check turns the mount 2 × 10°"), in both the full UI and Simple Mode. The check's *Last run said* figure, and its *Disagreement*, compare with the readout of the last run that ended with one. The Start card's *Skip the rough step* and *Check only* are replaced by **Start** and **Check**. Polar Align never moves the Dec axis.
