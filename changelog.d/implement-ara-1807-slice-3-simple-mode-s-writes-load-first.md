### Fixed

- **Simple Mode's camera cooler target, autofocus choices and telescope rows load the rig's settings before they save** (#1807): Simple Mode. A cooler target, an autofocus switch, preset, filter or exposure pick, or a telescope aperture or focal length now loads the rig's copy of those settings first, changes only that value in it and saves it, so nothing else on the rig is overwritten with a client default. When the rig cannot be reached, its copy cannot be loaded or another device saved a newer copy, nothing is written and a pill says why ("Settings not loaded from the rig; nothing was saved", "These settings changed on the rig; nothing was saved", or "Rig not connected" with no rig). A failed save puts the rig's value back. Two picks of the same settings in quick succession save one after the other, so the second never fails as a conflict with the first. Imaging defaults, optics and autofocus settings in Settings load from the rig, show Retry when they cannot, and read a reply that lacks a setting as a failed load instead of filling in a default; the offline copy is read-only and gives way to the rig's copy as soon as a rig is connected.
