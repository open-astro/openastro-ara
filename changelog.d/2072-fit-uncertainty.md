### Added (polar alignment)
- **Polar Align shows how sure its readout is** (Polar Align, #2072): the total error reads with its 1σ, for example "0.2′ ± 1.1′", in the full UI and Simple Mode. The σ comes from each point's frame scatter, carried through the three-point fit by linear propagation; the Check's verdict carries its own. A point is never counted surer than the solver can measure: 0.15 px at the solving camera's pixel scale (the guide or main camera, whichever took the point) per solve, and that floor over √n for a point of n frames; 1″ when the pixel scale is unknown, and the fit's log line says which floor it used. With tracking off, a hand-turn point's frames are first moved to their mean time, so the Earth's turn is not counted as scatter. The status, each `polar_align.progress`, the check result and `polar_align.checked` carry `altitude_sigma_arcmin`, `azimuth_sigma_arcmin` and the total's (`current_sigma_arcmin` on the status, `total_sigma_arcmin` elsewhere). The fit's log line has the σ with the axis and the three solved positions, and the session row keeps the final σ.
- **Polar Align says "inside your tolerance" only when the error plus twice its uncertainty is** (Polar Align, #2072): while the error alone is inside but not with twice its σ, the full UI, Simple Mode and the Polar Aligner's converged line say the measurement can't confirm the alignment and suggest larger turns (20° in place of 10° cuts the uncertainty about four times). Done stays available inside the tolerance, as before.

### Changed (polar alignment)
- **The Check's verdict rates the error plus twice its uncertainty** (Polar Align, #2072): the band ("Excellent" ≤ 1′, "Very good" ≤ 3′, …) in the Check's `verdict`, the full UI's badges and Simple Mode's checked line is taken on the total error plus twice its 1σ, so a noisy "0.2′ ± 1.3′" reads "Very good", not "Excellent". A single-frame check at 10° steps often reads one band lower than before; larger Check steps or more frames per point bring it back.

### Changed (guiding)
- **Polar Align and guiding disagree only by more than their combined uncertainty** (guiding, #2072): when tonight's Polar Align result carries its 1σ, the guiding residual is called a disagreement only when it is above Polar Align by more than twice √(σ_guiding² + σ_align²), in place of the fixed 2′ margin, and the tooltip shows both with it ("Polar Align and guiding disagree (12″ ± 66″ vs at least 17′ ± 70″)"). `PaResidualDto` gains `align_sigma_arcmin`. A Polar Align result from before this release keeps the 2′ margin.
