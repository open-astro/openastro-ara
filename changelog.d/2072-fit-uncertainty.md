### Breaking changes
- **Polar Align's Done waits for the error plus twice its uncertainty** (Polar Align, #2072): Done in the full UI and Simple Mode, and the rig's own check on a Done in the rough step (`POST /api/v1/equipment/polaralign/complete` with `stage: coarse`, 409), now need the total error plus twice its 1σ inside your tolerance. A readout that used to allow Done just inside the tolerance may now hold it while its measurement is noisy; one frame per point counts a 1″ floor per point, which at 10° turns is about 1.3′ of 1σ, and a point of n frames never counts under 1″ ÷ √n. At the defaults (1′ tolerance, 10° turns, 3 frames per point) that floor alone keeps Done waiting. **After upgrading:** if Done stays unavailable, set larger turns (Polar align turn and Check step, Settings → Imaging → Polar align: 20° in place of 10° cuts the uncertainty about four times), or raise the tolerance; more frames per point help a little.

### Added (polar alignment)
- **Polar Align shows how sure its readout is** (Polar Align, #2072): the total error reads with its 1σ, for example "0.2′ ± 1.1′", in the full UI and Simple Mode. The σ comes from each point's frame scatter (a single-frame point counts a 1″ floor, and an averaged point never less than that floor over √n; with tracking off, a hand-turn point's frames are first moved to their mean time, so the Earth's turn is not counted as scatter), carried through the three-point fit by linear propagation; the Check's verdict carries its own. The status, each `polar_align.progress`, the check result and `polar_align.checked` carry `altitude_sigma_arcmin`, `azimuth_sigma_arcmin` and the total's (`current_sigma_arcmin` on the status, `total_sigma_arcmin` elsewhere). The fit's log line has the σ with the axis and the three solved positions, and the session row keeps the final σ. While the error is inside the tolerance but not with twice its σ, the screen says why Done waits; Check stays available. With a Polar Aligner, the converged line says when Done still waits.

### Changed (guiding)
- **Polar Align and guiding disagree only by more than their combined uncertainty** (guiding, #2072): when tonight's Polar Align result carries its 1σ, the guiding residual is called a disagreement only when it is above Polar Align by more than twice √(σ_guiding² + σ_align²), in place of the fixed 2′ margin, and the tooltip shows both with it ("Polar Align and guiding disagree (12″ ± 66″ vs at least 17′ ± 70″)"). `PaResidualDto` gains `align_sigma_arcmin`. A Polar Align result from before this release keeps the 2′ margin.
