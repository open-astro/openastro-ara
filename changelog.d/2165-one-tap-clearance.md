### Changed (polar alignment)
- **Polar Align's clearance is one question, with no slider or angle** (#2165): the clearance dialog now says "Polar Align will turn the mount up to N° each way. Is there room for the tube, counterweight and cables to move that far?" with one button, **Yes, there is room**. N is how far this run turns RA each way, from the rig's plan: twice the turn size picked on the Start card, plus the 0.5° direction test when the RA motor's direction is not saved yet. The confirmation is kept per mount for that reach: a later start that turns further (a bigger turn size, or a Check whose Check step is bigger than the turn size) asks the question right there, on Start, then goes on; a smaller or equal one stays confirmed. The Start card always says "Polar align turns the mount up to N° each way.", and Settings › Imaging › Polar align shows it beside the confirmation. Take back stays, also for a confirmation smaller than the run. The counterweight-limit setting is gone; a confirmation saved in its old format is dropped, so that mount asks once more. API: `mount_clearance` entries are `{ confirmed_reach_deg }`, the pre-flight list's `planned.max_cw_angle_deg` is replaced by `planned.reach_deg`, and the `polar-align-clearance-not-confirmed` refusal carries `reach_deg`.
