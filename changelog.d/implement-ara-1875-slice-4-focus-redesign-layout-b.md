- **Focus is the Autofocus panel, with Tools rows** (#1875): Focus by hand (Bahtinov) and Filter offsets are Tools rows under Autofocus that open in place, with a Back arrow; the Auto / Manual / Offsets row and Focus's Info toggle are gone.
