### Changed
- **Simple Mode wheels: a tap on a row picks it** (Simple Mode, #2040). A tap on a row of the wheel under a Simple Mode tile sets that value and folds the wheel. A spin sets nothing until the wheel is folded with a tap on its tile, then sets the spun value once. The Done button is gone. The iOS and Android pickers, the keyboard and the screen-reader steps behave the same.
