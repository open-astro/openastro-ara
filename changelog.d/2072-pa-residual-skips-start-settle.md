### Fixed (guiding)
- **The guiding polar-alignment figure skips the start-guiding settle** (#2072): when guiding starts, the guider first pulls the star onto its lock position; those corrections are not drift, but nothing told Ara the settle had begun until its first progress report, and a guiding start forgot a settle the guider had already reported. Ara now hears the guider's settle-begin message (sent as the new `settle_begin` kind on `guider.event`) and leaves every frame out until the settle is done; a start the guider runs no settle for is still measured from its first frame.
- **The guiding polar-alignment figure no longer reads Dec backlash as drift** (#2072): when the Dec pulses change direction, the gear's backlash eats the first pulses and the star does not move. Ara now waits until the star answers the new pulses (at most 60 s) before it measures again. When the pulses go one way and the star keeps moving away from them anyway, the figure is marked unreliable, is not compared with Polar Align, and the guiding strip says so in its own words ("PA ≥ x · backlash": "Dec backlash ate the guide pulses, so this window can't measure polar alignment."), in English and Brazilian Portuguese, instead of "noisy". The daemon sends this as the new `dec_pulses_unanswered` field of the PA residual (also kept with each stored row); an app talking to an older daemon without it still shows "noisy". The User Guide explains both, and the 60 s wait after a Dec reversal. On the 2026-11-22 rig this window read ≥45.6′ against a true error of about 0.6′.
