### Fixed (mount)
- **A mount clock write that failed once is tried again, and Polar Align waits for the clock like GoTo** (#2071): when the mount refused Ara's clock write once at connect (a passing bridge error) and then could not report its clock, GoTo, Sync and Polar Align stayed refused for the whole session on a rig synced by NTP with no app open; the check every 30 s now writes the clock again from the synced time. Polar Align's Start now waits a few seconds for the clock Ara is setting just after the app connects, as GoTo does, instead of refusing at once. A Check pressed in the adjust step while the mount's clock is not verified is refused and leaves the adjust step running instead of ending it first. A GoTo sent in the instant the rig clock steps now waits for the clock to be set again instead of being refused.
