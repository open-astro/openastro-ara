### Fixed (mount)
- **Every slew target is checked before it reaches the mount** (#2075): a sequence, centering, meridian-flip or Polar Align slew with a Dec outside ±90° or a coordinate that is not a number now fails the step with a plain message, sends nothing to the mount and no longer raises a telescope fault that counts toward abort and park. A flip with such a target is refused before the side-of-pier hint. A mount that reports its Dec axis past the pole (an iOptron HAE29C read 90.9° after a sync) is folded to the same sky point, so a near-pole centering re-slew and the flip fallback target stay in range, and a goto to a folded target settles instead of ending in a stall fault.
