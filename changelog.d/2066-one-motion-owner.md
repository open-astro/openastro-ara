### Breaking changes
- **Polar Align holds the mount for its whole run: GoTo, Center, Park and Home are refused until it ends** (Polar Align, #2066, #2065): `POST /equipment/telescope/slew` (not a sync), `/park`, `/home` and `POST /plate-solve/center` used to be refused with `polar-align-moving-mount` only while a Polar Align motor move ran; they are now refused from Start until the run has ended, its solves and the live adjust loop included, and a sequence's GoTo fails the same way. A direction-pad move keeps the narrower rule. **After upgrading:** a client or script that moves the mount during Polar Align stops Polar Align (or taps Done, or sends Stop Mount) first.
- **Polar Align refuses to start on a slewing mount or a guiding guider** (Polar Align, #2066): a start whose mount slews (or gives no Slewing answer), or whose guider guides or calibrates, is refused with the new Refusal cause `polar-align-mount-busy` (a busy guider was a plain 409 before). **After upgrading:** let the slew finish or stop guiding, then start Polar Align; a client that matched the guider's 409 message matches the new cause.

### Fixed (polar alignment)
- **A GoTo can no longer run beside a Polar Align turn** (Polar Align, #2066, #2065, #2048): a GoTo accepted while Polar Align solved a frame between its two turns used to run while the next turn drove the RA motor. Polar Align now takes the mount's single motion owner for its whole run (Joey's decision on #2048) and gives it back once its exit has stopped the mount. Before each turn it reads the mount twice, 1 s apart: if the mount slews or moved by other means (the hand controller, another program), or the guider started guiding or calibrating, the run ends with `mount_busy` before the motor starts. Emergency Stop, Stop Mount, a pad stop, tracking off and the safety, fault and shutdown parks are never refused.
