### Added (tests)
- **Polar Align tests can see what the mount really did** (#2064): a fake German equatorial mount (`OpenAstroAra.Test/PolarAlign/FakeGemMount`) keeps its true mechanical pose (RA axis, Dec axis past the pole, counterweight angle, pier side, tracking) apart from what it reports, and records every motion, stop and forbidden call in a ledger. It models leased MoveAxis moves, a counterweight-down firmware GoTo with soft limits and the AlpacaBridge 4.x `:MS2#` counterweight-up re-aim, and the reporting lies of #2048 (driver cache, wrong mount clock, Slewing lies, ignored stops, a false zero after a power cycle). A shared check applies #2048's five common asserts to any run. The key Stop, failure and sweep scenarios of Polar Align now also run on it and assert on the true pose, and the existing Polar Align mount fixtures state their pier side and LST.
