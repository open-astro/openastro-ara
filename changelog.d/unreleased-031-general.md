### Added
- **§48.7 — generated flats obey your calibration settings, and they auto-expose.** The end-of-night matching-flats sequences now emit the new Flat Panel Flats step per filter (probe to your target ADU, then capture the set) instead of a fixed 1-second exposure loop with an advisory target — and the target ADU, tolerance, frames per filter, and "park the mount after flats" all live under **Settings → Session → Calibration** and are enforced, not suggestions. The step also appears in the sequence editor's new **Calibration** category for hand-built plans.
- **§48.3 — flats that expose themselves correctly.** A new **Flat Panel Flats** sequence instruction captures a whole flat set for the current filter: it lights the connected flat panel at your chosen brightness, probes exposures until the frame's mean brightness hits the target ADU (default 30000 ± 5%), then takes the requested number of FLAT frames (default 30) at the converged exposure — through the same pipeline as normal captures, so they land in the library with FITS files. No panel driver? It still works — the probe measures whatever light source you point at (manual EL panel, sky) and fails honestly if the scene is dark or the target is unreachable within the exposure bounds. The panel light is always restored off, even on cancel. (The §39.5 generated matching-flats sequence switching to this instruction, sky-flat twilight timing, and the profile calibration blocks are the arc's next slices.)
- **§36 — highlight whole catalogs on the sky atlas.** The planetarium's new **Catalogs** drawer overlays colored rings for every object of a chosen catalog (Messier, Caldwell, NGC, IC) or type (galaxies, globular clusters, planetary nebulae, …) — the brightest 500 of each, ring size scaled by magnitude so showpieces read at a glance. Toggle any mix; each catalog keeps its own color, your picks survive restarts, and the rings sit over the normal deep-sky layer so names and tap-to-select still work. Requires the OpenNGC sky-data package (the drawer says so honestly when it's missing).
- **§43 — backups now carry your image library's records.** Every backup ZIP includes a consistent snapshot of the frames catalog (`db/openastroara.db` — sessions, frame metadata, ratings, tags; never the FITS files themselves, which back up via drive clone). The snapshot is taken safely while captures run, and the backup's manifest reports how many frame records it holds. Restoring gains an opt-in **Frame catalog (metadata)** checkbox: it rolls the library's records back to the snapshot — deliberately unchecked by default, since frames captured after the snapshot would vanish from the library (their FITS files stay on disk and can be re-registered by the startup scan).

### Changed
- **§43 — a corrupt backup archive now surfaces through the restore status, not the initial request.** The integrity check moved onto the restore worker (archives can carry a sizeable catalog snapshot now), so the restore request accepts immediately and a checksum failure appears as the restore's failed outcome in WILMA — live config is still never touched.
