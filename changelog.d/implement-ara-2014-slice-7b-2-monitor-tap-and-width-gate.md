### Added (Simple Mode)

- **A tap enlarges a Monitor tile** (#2014): the tile fills the grid, clear of Emergency Stop's corner, and a second tap shrinks it; a long press opens the window that owns the tile, and a screen reader gets "Enlarge" or "Shrink" with a separate "Open" action. The Safety strip does not enlarge: its tap opens the Safety monitor window. A Conditions long press opens the Weather window, or with no station the Camera window, or the Focuser window when only the focuser reports.

### Changed (Simple Mode)

- **Monitor opens at any window width** (#2014): the left rail's Monitor item is no longer left out under 900 pt; a narrow window gets the one-column layout a phone has. Tiles for absent gear are not built and the grid reflows without a hole, and the Now tile shows a busy exposure whole on an iPad (1024 x 768 and 1133 x 744).

### Fixed (Simple Mode)

- **Monitor's Conditions tile fits in Brazilian Portuguese** (#2014): three long labels are shorter ("Acima do orvalho", "Resfriador", "Autofoco") and a label wraps instead of being cut, so the full rig no longer scrolls at 1024 x 768 and 1133 x 744.
