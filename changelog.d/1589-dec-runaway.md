### Fixed (guider)

- **Guiding stops when Dec runs away** (#1589): when the Dec error keeps growing for 10 guide frames in a row while the Dec pulses sit at their maximum and PHD2 cuts them to its Max Dec Duration (for example a wrong Dec direction after a meridian flip), Ara stops guiding and publishes a guider fault (`tracking_lost`, details `dec_runaway`). Your "When the guider is lost" policy then pauses, skips or aborts the run, and a notification tells you to calibrate the guider again. Settle frames count, so a runaway right after a flip is caught within about 20 s. Once guiding is started again and has settled, a paused run resumes on its own. A normal settle, a dither, a short gust, a steady drift at the pulse limit and Dec backlash (growing pulses below the limit) do not trip it. Before, the runaway was only noticed by the polar alignment residual and guiding carried on.
