### Fixed (guider)

- **Guiding stops when Dec runs away** (#1589): when the Dec error keeps growing for 10 guide frames in a row while the Dec pulses sit at their maximum (for example a wrong Dec direction after a meridian flip), Ara stops guiding and publishes a guider fault (`tracking_lost`, details `dec_runaway`). Your "When the guider is lost" policy then pauses, skips or aborts the run, and a notification tells you to calibrate the guider again. Settle frames count, so a runaway right after a flip is caught within about 20 s. Once guiding is started again and has settled, a paused run resumes on its own. A normal settle, a dither, a short gust and a steady drift at the pulse limit do not trip it. Before, the runaway was only noticed by the polar alignment residual and guiding carried on.
