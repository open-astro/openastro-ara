### Fixed (guider)

- **Losing the guider mid-frame stops the frame and brings guiding back** (#2169): when the guider link drops or the guide camera drops out while guiding, the light frame in progress now stops at once instead of finishing unguided, and it is not saved or counted. The run pauses, and once the guider is back Ara starts guiding again and waits for it to settle before the run resumes and takes the frame again, instead of waiting for the next dither. If the guider drops again during that restart, Ara waits for it once more. If guiding does not restart (the camera is still gone, or clouds), the run stays paused and Ara keeps trying (after 30 s, 1 min, then every 2 min) until it works or you take the run over; one notice says it is still trying. A guide camera drop now ends only when real guide frames come back, not when the guider merely says the camera is back. A lost guide star works the same way, as before, and darks and bias now always finish.
