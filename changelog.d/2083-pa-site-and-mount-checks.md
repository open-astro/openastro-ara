### Fixed (polar alignment)
- **Polar Align refuses an unset site, an alt-az mount and a mount that cannot turn RA by motor speed** (#2083): like NINA, a start is refused before anything moves when the site has no longitude, still sits at latitude 0° and longitude 0° (a site never set), or its sidereal time cannot be worked out (`polar-align-site-not-set`); when the mount reports it is alt-az, where turning its first axis turns azimuth, not RA (`polar-align-alt-az-mount`); and when the mount reports it cannot turn its RA axis by rate, `CanMoveAxis` false (`polar-align-cannot-move-axis`). The pre-flight list on the Polar Align card (Full UI and Simple Mode) now fills its site longitude and sidereal time, mount type and RA motor rows, and the app words each refusal in English and Portuguese (Brazil). A mount that does not report its type or its `CanMoveAxis` is not refused.
- **The Polar Aligner pre-flight row passes with its motors on** (#2083): it read "Not checked yet"; the aligner's Stop is now verified and its turns capped (#2069), so the row says so and shows as passed.

### Removed (polar alignment)
- **The Polar Align pre-flight list drops its five "Not checked yet" rows** (#2083): the AlpacaBridge motion-safety, pier side, start-near-the-pole, start counterweight angle and turn step rows waited for a change (#2065) that is not coming (Ara follows NINA here), so they are gone from the list. An older app shows the shorter list as it is.
