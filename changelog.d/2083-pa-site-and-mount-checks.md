### Fixed (polar alignment)
- **Polar Align refuses an unset site, an alt-az mount and a mount that cannot turn RA by motor speed** (#2083): like NINA, a start is refused before anything moves when the site has no longitude, still sits at latitude 0° and longitude 0° (a site never set), or its sidereal time cannot be worked out (`polar-align-site-not-set`); when the mount reports it is alt-az, where turning its first axis turns azimuth, not RA (`polar-align-alt-az-mount`); and, for a motor start, when the mount reports it cannot turn its RA axis by rate, `CanMoveAxis` false (`polar-align-cannot-move-axis`; **Turn RA by hand** still starts, with a warning). The pre-flight list on the Polar Align card (Full UI and Simple Mode) now fills its site longitude and sidereal time, mount type and RA motor rows, and the app words each refusal in English and Portuguese (Brazil). A mount that does not report its type or its `CanMoveAxis` is not refused.
