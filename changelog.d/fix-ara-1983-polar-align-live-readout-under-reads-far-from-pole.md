### Fixed

- **The Polar Align live readout no longer under-reads an adjustment far from the pole** (Polar Align, #1983): the fine and rough stages mapped the camera's alt/az shift to the polar axis's shift one for one, true only near the pole, so a camera 30° from it read about 0.73 of an altitude move and the routine (and a Polar Aligner) could stop early; the readout now maps the shift back through the small-angle model of a rigid mount (`PolarAlignGeometry.AxisShiftFromPointingShift`), within 2 % at 5°, 15° and 30° from the pole at a northern and a southern site (`PolarAlignGeometryTest.The_live_readout_reads_each_axis_move_within_2_percent_far_from_the_pole`). The cos ψ floor keeps its sign, so a camera past ψ = ±90° (latitude 60° or more) no longer reads the altitude knob with the wrong sign (`PolarAlignGeometryTest` rows 70° and -70°).
