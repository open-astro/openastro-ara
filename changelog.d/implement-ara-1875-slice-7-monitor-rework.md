### Changed (Simple Mode)

- **Monitor reworked** (#1875, slice 7): the Last frame tile is now an HFR tile and a Stars tile with the sky background, each a graph with one point per saved frame, and Weather is a Conditions tile that also carries the camera sensor, cooler power and focuser temperatures. A reading that moved 15 % the wrong way against the session's own start (the median of its first three saved frames against the last three; HFR up, stars down, sky background up) gets a flag such as "HFR +18 % since session start", and none shows before six saved frames. The Guider tile draws the live trace on a fixed ±2.5″ scale with a labelled axis and an RA / Dec key, and the whole session's guiding RMS per saved frame from the existing `/stats/guiding` read. Target altitude shades the imaging span under the curve with a dot for now, a dashed 30° line and the plan's start and end on the axis, and Tonight shows hour marks and the flip and park times. Status words sit in the right corner of each tile header, and Monitor has a title with its icon.
- **A tap on a Monitor tile enlarges it inside Monitor** and a second tap shrinks it; the enlarged tile has a button to the window the tap used to open. Monitor is offered on windows narrower than 900 pt too, in its one column. With no Safety monitor and no weather station their tiles are not drawn and the grid reflows (Target altitude takes two columns, HFR and Stars two rows, the flip sits beside Tonight). Tonight's Best is gone from Monitor; Plan keeps it.

### Fixed (Simple Mode)

- **Monitor's frame and filter text** (#1875): a camera with no filter shows no filter text or separator ("NGC 1269 · 60 s", "Frame 28"), "Frame 27 of 120" is the frame being exposed beside "26 saved" and no bare "26/120", a long name equal to the short one once spaces are removed ("NGC 1269" and "NGC1269") is shown once, the frames line no longer repeats the target, and the autofocus line ends "since then" instead of a dangling "since". The Now tile's countdown keeps its own width and Up next takes the rest, so a wide window has no empty area between them.
