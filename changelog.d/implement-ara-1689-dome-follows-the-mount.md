### Added (dome follows the mount)

- **The dome follows the mount** (#1689). With **Follows the mount** on, Ara turns the dome to keep its slit in front of the scope after a mount slew, after a meridian flip, and while tracking whenever the slit falls behind by more than the tolerance; exposures and the sequencer's Synchronize dome wait for the dome to arrive. Turn it on in Simple Mode's Dome window (it shows "Following the mount…" while it turns) or in the full UI's Dome panel, which also takes the dome geometry (radius, scope position, mount axis offsets, tolerance); a scope outside the dome is refused with a reason. Turning the dome by hand, parking it, sending it home, stopping it, disconnecting it, an unsafe-weather reaction, an emergency stop, or a geometry that puts the scope outside the dome turns following off; a dropped connection leaves it on, and following resumes when the dome reconnects. With following off, the dome still turns once to the scope after a meridian flip and after each centering re-slew, once the dome radius is set. The setting is per profile and kept across restarts; sharing a profile leaves its dome geometry and follow setting out, and the import lists them to set again. New `POST /equipment/dome/follow`, `GET/PUT /profile/dome` and `runtime.following` on the dome status.
