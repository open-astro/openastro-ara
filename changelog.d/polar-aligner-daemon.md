### Added (equipment)
- **Polar Aligner: the daemon drives a motorised polar alignment device** (Polar Align, #1971): a connected Alpaca Switch or mount whose driver offers the interim `PolarAlign:*` actions (OAPA, OpenAstroTracker, the PolarAlign simulator) is the rig's Polar Aligner. In the live stages the Polar Align Routine makes a small test move on each axis, then corrects 80 % of the error after each measurement until it is inside the tolerance. Any refusal, fault, timeout, lost link, wrong direction or growing error stops the motors and the Routine carries on by hand. New: `GET /equipment/polaraligner` with stop and home, `polar_align.aligner`, stop and resume in a running Routine, the profile switch `polar_aligner_auto`, and an Emergency Stop rung. The app side follows in its own change.
