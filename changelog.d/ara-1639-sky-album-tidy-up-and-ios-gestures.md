### Added (Sky Album gestures)

- **iOS pull to refresh.** On iPhone and iPad, pulling the Sky Album down fetches what the rig changed, and pulling the rig list down rescans, with the system's own refresh control (#1634).
- **iOS context menus.** On iPhone and iPad, a long press on a Sky Album Card or a queued plan target opens the system context menu with the actions it already has: Image this target or Add more time on a Card, Edit and Remove on a target. Other platforms are unchanged (#1635).

### Changed (Sky Album)

- **Nights by the site's clock.** A Card's Nights follow the site's longitude (the time at the site), not the device's time zone, so a tablet in another zone no longer splits one night in two (#1627).

### Fixed (Sky Album)

- **Art credit link.** The Art credit's licence link is announced as a link to screen readers, and a licence page that can't be opened says so instead of doing nothing (#1639).
