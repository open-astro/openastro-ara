### Fixed (polar alignment)
- **A Polar Align stop reaches a mount that dropped off once it reconnects, and a stuck run can no longer abort a park** (#2066, #2048): a stop to a mount that was not connected gave up at once and reported "cut the mount's power"; it now goes out as soon as the mount connects again, and is confirmed as usual, inside the same 8 s. A Stop Mount that landed while one of Polar Align's own stop commands was refused could be taken for Polar Align's own stop, and the next run then turned the mount after it; only the stops Polar Align itself sent count as its own now. When Emergency Stop, the safety park or the equipment-fault park has to wait for a stuck polar alignment, Polar Align now answers within 9 s and sends no further stop of that run, so a late stop no longer aborts the park (and Emergency Stop no longer reports its polar-alignment step as failed). A run that a Stop gives up on sends nothing when it finally unwinds, and with "stop tracking when done" off, the Done that gives up on it hands back the tracking it found itself. A tap on the direction pad during a turn now ends the run: the motor is no longer started again by the routine's next renewal. A GoTo whose command was still on its way when Emergency Stop or Stop Mount landed is stopped again, unless a park, Find Home or another GoTo (yours or the sequence's) was sent after the last stop: that move now goes on. If the mount refuses that newer move, the GoTo is still stopped.
