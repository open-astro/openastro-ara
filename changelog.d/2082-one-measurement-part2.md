### Breaking changes
- **The app needs a rig that knows the Polar Align camera and frames per point** (Polar Align, #2082): the polar-align profile section has two new keys, `camera` and `frames_per_point`, and the app reads them from the rig with no default of its own (ADR 0015). Against an older rig, the app shows the Polar Align settings as not loaded and saves none of them. `PUT /api/v1/profile/polar-align` now refuses a `camera` other than `guide` or `main` with 400; a body without the key keeps the guide camera. **After upgrading:** update the rig with the app.

### Added (polar alignment)
- **Polar Align camera** (Polar Align, #2082): Settings → Imaging → Polar align → *Camera* picks the camera that measures the axis, runs the live readout and takes the Check: the guide camera (the default, as before) or the main camera, with its own plate solver at the main camera exposure. Start refuses while the chosen main camera is not connected, before anything moves. The `polar_align.started` event and the status carry the run's `camera`. A run on the main camera shows no guide-camera picture (the guider's last frame is not this run's).
- **Frames per point** (Polar Align, #2082): each of a measurement's three points (the seed and the Check alike) now takes *Frames per point* frames with the mount still (1 to 5, 3 by default) and averages them as unit vectors, so one bad solve no longer moves the axis. The log has each point's mean, its rms scatter in arcseconds and the number of failed solves. A point scattered over 3″ is retaken once; if the retake is still scattered, or does not solve at all (a cloud: the first frames then stand for the point), the run goes on and the result is marked noisy (`measurement_noisy` on the status and each `polar_align.progress` event, `noisy` on the check result), with a caution under the readout and under the Check in both the full UI and Simple Mode. A measurement now takes about three times as many frames by default; set *Frames per point* to 1 for the old single frame.
