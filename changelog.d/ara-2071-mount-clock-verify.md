### Breaking changes
- **GoTo, Sync, Center and polar alignment wait for a verified mount clock** (#2071): until the rig clock is trusted (a time sync from the app connecting or a GPS fix it relays, or the kernel reporting the clock NTP-synchronised) and the mount's clock has been set from it and checked, these answer 409 `mount-clock-not-verified` and a sequencer slew fails its step. **After upgrading:** on a rig with working NTP nothing changes; otherwise open the app once after the rig boots (it pushes the time on connect), or sync the time in Settings, before the first GoTo. Park and Home are not affected.

### Fixed (mount)
- **Time sync sets the mount's clock, not only the Pi's** (#2071): on RC51 the §31 push corrected the Pi but the mount kept the time AlpacaBridge had sent from the stale boot clock, 6 h 48 min slow, so every GoTo pointed about 102° off. After a trusted time sync and on every mount connect, Ara now writes the mount's `UTCDate` from the synced time, logs the mount's UTC before and after, and checks the mount's `UTCDate` and `SiderealTime` against the synced clock and the LST computed for the profile's site. The check runs again after a rig clock step, after a reconnect (with no one-hour expiry) and, while it fails, about every 30 s. A driver that cannot report `UTCDate` is checked by its sidereal time. A GoTo or Sync sent while the app's time sync is still setting the mount's clock waits for it (up to 10 s) instead of being refused, and a slow bridge's round trips no longer count as clock error. A rig whose kernel reports its clock NTP-synchronised (`adjtimex`: `STA_UNSYNC` clear and an error bound within 2 s) is trusted without the app, so after a daemon restart the mount's clock is set and the GoTo gate opens on connect; an unsynchronised kernel clock is never written to the mount.
